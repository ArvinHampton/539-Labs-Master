That is the wrong condition.

Hypotheses. a, b, c positive integers. a c less than b squared. b divisible by a. b divisible by c.

The condition is a divides b and c divides b. It is not a c divides b.

For (2, 8, 8).
Sixteen less than sixty-four. Eight divisible by two. Eight divisible by eight.
Theorem 0.4 applies.

For (2, 7, 7). Seven is not divisible by two. Theorem 0.4 does not apply.
