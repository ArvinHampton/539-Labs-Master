## 1. Experimental Summary

High-resolution collinear resonance ionization spectroscopy (CRIS) at CERN-ISOLDE measured the ground-state spin and nuclear magnetic dipole moment of the radioactive isotope 61Cr (N=37, Z=24).

- Ground-state spin firmly established as I = 1/2 (revising the previously adopted tentative assignment I = (5/2)).
- Magnetic dipole moment measured as mu(61Cr) = +0.541(6) mu_N.
- The positive sign and magnitude lie close to the single-particle value of a nu p1/2 neutron (+0.638 mu_N), supporting negative parity and the configuration I^pi = 1/2^-.
- The hyperfine spectrum shows a clear two-peak structure consistent only with I = 1/2; higher-spin hypotheses are excluded by chi-squared and peak counting.

The measurement revises the interpretation of existing beta-decay data for 61V to 61Cr and 61Cr to 61Mn and requires re-evaluation of the low-lying level scheme of 61Cr (tentative assignments of (3/2^-) and (5/2^-) for the 70.8 keV and 97.7 keV states respectively).
