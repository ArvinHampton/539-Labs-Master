## Spin-orbit

This coupling does not need torsion. It is L·S, from the electromagnetic field or from the strong field inside a nucleus, or, in a curved metric, from the Riemann tensor acting on spin.

Electromagnetic, hydrogen-like scale for Z = 6: (Z alpha)^2 times 13.6 eV = 0.026 eV. A valence electron in an organic molecule sits below that. It is not 0.6 meV by identity, and it is not the lock.

Nuclear shell spin-orbit is a mega-electron-volt scale. It is not the stipulated contact term.

Gravitational spin-curvature, Mathisson-Papapetrou order: hbar c times Riemann times a Compton length. At the 137 nm warp rod, k = 1.85 times 10^5 per metre and Riemann is k^2. With the nucleon Compton length 2.10 times 10^{-16} m, the scale is 1.42 times 10^{-12} eV. Smaller than 0.6 meV by 8.6 decades. Longer than 18 fs as a period by a wide margin. It uses the warp curvature, not the torsion. The lock torsion remains zero.

Multiplying hbar c by k alone gives 0.036 eV. That product is not the coupling. It inserts an inverse length as if it were a torsion. Refused.
