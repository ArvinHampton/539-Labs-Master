Warped Brane Inflation Note
Category B exploratory continuum note. Residual discrete algebra remains untouched and Category A. Residual-flux provenance mandatory.

Warped brane inflation is driven by the slow motion of a D3-brane (or a D3–anti-D3 pair) inside a strongly warped throat produced by fluxes. The warp factor exponentially suppresses energy scales near the tip of the throat, flattening the inter-brane potential enough to permit a prolonged slow-roll phase.

The radial separation of the mobile brane acts as the inflaton. In the Klebanov–Strassler-type throat the Coulomb attraction is sufficiently diluted that tens of e-folds become possible before the branes annihilate or reach the tip.

DBI variants allow relativistic motion and non-canonical kinetic terms, permitting inflation on steeper potentials. All such constructions remain continuum-level and Category B. They do not modify the residual discrete algebra, the flux-budget counting, or the HQCC termination count of 539 ± 1 steps.
