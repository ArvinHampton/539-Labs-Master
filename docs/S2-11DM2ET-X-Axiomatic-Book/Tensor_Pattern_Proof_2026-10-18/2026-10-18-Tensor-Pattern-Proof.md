# Proof of the tensor pattern for W = Fix(P) (Theorem F representation), 2026-10-18
Folder 2026-10-18-Tensor-Pattern-Proof/: disc.py/.txt (exact), local.py/.txt and extra.py/.txt (numerical certificates), family.py. Local only; nothing pushed.

## Theorem
Let ρ be the representation of PSL₂(Z) on W (dim 72), with S² = (ST)³ = I. Then:
1. ρ factors through SL₂(Z/140) = SL₂(Z/4) × SL₂(F₅) × SL₂(F₇), and ρ = φ₄ ⊗ φ₅ ⊗ φ₇ on commuting local images. Each local image is the full group: orders 48, 120 and 336.
2. W ≅ (A₃ ⊕ B₁) ⊗ (W₅⁺⊗W₇⁺ ⊕ W₅⁻⊗W₇⁻). Here W₅ is the Weil representation of q₅ = 2x²/5 on Z/5 (± halves of dims 3 and 2), and W₇ is the Weil representation of q₇ = x²/7 on Z/7 (halves of dims 4 and 3). A₃ and B₁ are the irreducibles of SL₂(Z/4) described below.
3. The mixed-parity pieces W₅^±⊗W₇^∓ are absent because P = ρ(−I) acts on (A₃ or B₁)⊗W₅^s⊗W₇^t by the scalar s·t.
4. V_Eis = A₃⊗X⁺ (36), V_cusp = B₁⊗X⁻ (6), V^⊥ = B₁⊗X⁺ (12) ⊕ A₃⊗X⁻ (18).

## Proof outline and status
**Step 1a: local structure of the finite quadratic module (Cat A, exact).**
- L′/Z² ≅ Z/35, generated by 70g = (14,6). B(kg,kg) = 2k²·13/70, and the CRT split is orthogonal: B(g₅,g₇) = 0 for g₅ = 7g and g₇ = 5g.
- B(g₅,g₅) = 1/5 and B(g₇,g₇) ≡ 2/7. The odd lattice makes Q defined only mod ½ on L′/Z²; the w_c twist carries the 2-adic half.
- From the T-spectra (Step 2), the local T exponents are x₅ ∈ {0,2,3} and x₇ ∈ {0,1,2,4}, i.e. 2x²/5 and x²/7.
- Correction to V42-Structure.md: its table listed raw residues a mod p of the 140th-root exponent a. The true local exponents satisfy a = 35x₄ + 28x₅ + 20x₇, so x₄ = 3a mod 4, x₅ = 2a mod 5 and x₇ = 6a mod 7.

**Step 1b: congruence factorization (Cat B, numerically certified; the logic is exact given the checks).**
- Explicit M_p ∈ SL₂(Z) with M_p ≡ S mod p^k and M_p ≡ I off p:
  - M₄ = [[90476, 151655], [105, 176]]
  - M₅ = [[68125, 103404], [56, 85]]
  - M₇ = [[−40159, −53880], [120, 161]]
- Each ρ(M_p) is evaluated through an explicit S,T word. The local T generators are T^{105}, T^{56} and T^{120} (the CRT idempotents).
- Checks:
  - ρ(S) = ∏ρ(M_p) (3e-13).
  - Local generators at different primes commute (5e-14).
- Each pair group (ρ(g), model(g)) has exactly |SL₂(Z/p^k)| elements: 48 against matrices mod 4, 120 against the Weil model of 2x²/5, and 336 against the Weil model of x²/7. These models are faithful, so each φ_p is a well-defined homomorphism on SL₂(Z/p^k).
- Then φ = φ₄φ₅φ₇ is a homomorphism on SL₂(Z/140) that agrees with ρ on S and T, so ρ factors through it.
- To upgrade this to Cat A: redo the same finite checks in exact Q(ζ₁₄₀) arithmetic, or cite the congruence theorem for Weil representations.

**Step 2: local irreducibles (Cat B for the numerics; the identification is exact once the characters are known).**
- W|SL₂(F₅) = 16·W₅⁺ + 12·W₅⁻, and W|SL₂(F₇) = 12·W₇⁺ + 8·W₇⁻. Character residuals are 2e-11, and the Weil halves have norm 1 (irreducible).
- W|SL₂(Z/4) = 18·A₃ + 18·B₁. Residual 6e-12; norms 1; ⟨A₃,B₁⟩ = 0.
- B₁ is the character of SL₂(Z/4) with T ↦ i and S ↦ i: an order-4 character factoring through the abelianisation Z/12.
- A₃ is the 3-dim irreducible with T-spectrum {1, i, −1} and central character χ(−I) = −3. Its values on SL₂(Z/4) lie in {±3, ±1, ±i, 0}.
- Interpreting A₃ ⊕ B₁ as the 2-adic (odd-lattice, w_c-twisted) Weil representation is Cat B and not derived.

**Step 3: decomposition and P-invariance.**
- The joint isotypic projectors E₄E₅E₇ (built from the full local groups, so they commute) have ranks 36 / 6 / 12 / 18 on the four matched pieces and **0 on all four mixed pieces** (Cat B, numerical).
- Why the mixed pieces vanish (Cat A given Step 2):
  - P = ρ(S)² = ∏ρ(M_p)², and M_p² is the central element −I at p.
  - Central characters: −I acts by −1 on both A₃ and B₁ (since B₁(S)² = i² = −1 and χ_A(−I) = −3).
  - In the Weil models, S² = +Z on W₅ and S² = −Z on W₇. So −I acts on W₅^± by ±1 and on W₇^± by ∓1. These are standard quadratic Gauss-sum signs, confirmed numerically.
  - Hence P acts on (A or B)⊗W₅^s⊗W₇^t by (−1)·s·(−t) = s·t, and P = I on W forces s = t.
  - Measured on V: V_Eis has (ε₄,ε₅,ε₇) = (−1,+1,−1) and V_cusp has (−1,−1,+1), as predicted.

**Step 4: V is exactly the matched-diagonal pieces (Cat B).**
- The isotypic projectors restricted to V_Eis, V_cusp and V^⊥ give the ranks in the theorem exactly. A₃⊗X⁺ lies inside V_Eis, B₁⊗X⁻ inside V_cusp, and the other two pieces inside V^⊥.
- That the lift annihilates B₁⊗X⁺ and A₃⊗X⁻ is shown only numerically: the Fourier rank is 42 at |Q| ≤ 25 and ≤ 60.
- **Open (not proven):** an exact cancellation argument (γ-phase flip or sign-of-norm) for these two pieces.

**Step 5: why Eisenstein ↔ A₃⊗even⊗even and cusp ↔ B₁⊗odd⊗odd (Cat B, interpretation, untested).**
- The odd Weil halves at 5 and 7 carry theta series whose local characters are odd at both p = 5 and p = 7.
- By the product formula, a character of Q(√35) that is odd at both 5 and 7 must also have a nontrivial sign at the infinite places. That matches the infinity-type tie θ_{c,0} ↔ θ_{c,1} found for the cusp forms (V42 §2).
- The genus (Eisenstein) characters are even at 5 and 7 and have trivial infinity type, so they land in X⁺.
- Which mod-4 factor accompanies each (A₃ or B₁) is fixed by the central-sign constraint of Step 3 together with which pieces the lift realises. Its 2-adic arithmetic meaning was not derived.
