## 2. Layer (F) — residual form cochain complex

### 2.1 Generators

Work over \(\mathbb{Q}\) on the **ordered** residual product cells (charge labels \(a<b<c\); path indices increasing).

| Degree | Basis elements | Evaluation |
|--------|----------------|------------|
| 0 | \(\mathbf{1}\), \(f\) | constant; tower height \(f(i)=\tau(x_i)\) on core 0 |
| 1 | \(h_\alpha\), \(v_{\delta}\), \(h_{\alpha f}\) | \(h_\alpha(a,b;i)=\alpha(a,b)\); \(v_{\delta}(a;i)=\delta f(i)\); \(h_{\alpha f}(a,b;i)=\alpha(a,b)f(i)\) |
| 2 | \(t_\mu\), \(t_\omega\), \(s_{\alpha\delta}\), \(t_{\omega f}\) | \(\mu\), \(\omega_2\) on triangles; \(\alpha\,\delta f\) on squares; \(\omega_2 f\) on triangles |

### 2.2 Differentials (from product \(D\))

\[
\begin{aligned}
D\mathbf{1}&=0,
&
Df&=v_{\delta},
\\
Dh_\alpha&=t_\omega,
&
Dv_{\delta}&=0,
&
Dh_{\alpha f}&=-s_{\alpha\delta}+t_{\omega f},
\\
D\big|_{F^2}&=0
&\text{(all listed 2-generators are cocycles)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

(Consistent with \(D(\alpha\otimes f)=-\eta\) and \(D(\alpha\otimes\delta f)=\omega_2\otimes\delta f\).)

### 2.3 Ordered-triple relation

On ordered charge triples \(a<b<c\),
\[
\mu(a,b,c)=B',
\qquad
\omega_2(a,b,c)=-1,
\qquad
\mu=-B'\,\omega_2.
\]
Hence \(t_\mu+B' t_\omega=0\) on the ordered form complex, and
\[
t_\mu=-B'\,t_\omega=D(-B' h_\alpha)
\]
is **exact**.

### 2.4 Cohomology of the form complex

**Theorem (Form cohomology).**  
Over \(\mathbb{Q}\), for the residual form complex \(F^\bullet\) above,
\[
\begin{aligned}
H^0(F)
&\cong
\mathbb{Q}
&&\text{generated by }[\mathbf{1}],
\\
H^1(F)
&\cong
0,
\\
H^2(F)
&\cong
\mathbb{Q}
&&\text{generated by }
[s_{\alpha\delta}]
=
[\alpha\otimes\delta f],
\end{aligned}
\]
and
\[
[\eta]=0,
\qquad
[\tilde\mu]=0,
\qquad
[t_\omega]=0
\]
in \(H^2(F)\).

*Proof sketch.*  
\(H^0\): \(\ker D=\mathrm{span}\{\mathbf{1}\}\) since \(Df=v_{\delta}\neq 0\).  
\(H^1\): \(\ker D=\mathrm{span}\{v_{\delta}\}=\mathrm{im}(D:\ F^0\to F^1)\).  
\(H^2\): \(\mathrm{im} D^1=\mathrm{span}\{t_\omega,\ s_{\alpha\delta}-t_{\omega f}\}\).  
With \(t_\mu=-B't_\omega\), a basis of \(F^2/\mathrm{im}\) is \([s_{\alpha\delta}]\); the class is nontrivial because any expression of \(s_{\alpha\delta}\) through \(\mathrm{im} D^1\) forces \(f\) constant on triangles vs squares (probe + linear algebra). ∎

**Interpretation.**  
The **only** residual product 2-class in the form package, up to exact terms, is the **cup / tensor coupling** of sector orientation \(\alpha\) with residual tower transport \(\delta f\):
\[
\boxed{
[s_{\alpha\delta}]
\in
H^2(F)
\cong
\mathbb{Q}.
}
\]
The previously locked \(\eta\) is cohomologically trivial (\(D(\alpha\otimes f)=-\eta\)), as is \(\tilde\mu\) on the ordered complex.
