- Published multi-frequency study of PKS 2233-148 (Pushkarev et al., MNRAS 2019; arXiv:1808.06138): Fermi-LAT light curve constructed with 7-day bins (Pass 8, 100 MeV–300 GeV).

- 540 s = 9 minutes.

All public pre-binned products are coarser than ~540 s by more than an order of magnitude. However:

Status: event-level data exist and support custom analysis in principle, but the public record does not supply the required sub-hour observables. Extracting them would require a new dedicated analysis and is outside the present measurement-design lock.
