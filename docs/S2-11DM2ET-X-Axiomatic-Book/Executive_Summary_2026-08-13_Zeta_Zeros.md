# Executive Summary — Riemann zeta zeros (2026-08-13)

Zeta has two families of zeros. The trivial zeros at the negative even integers are completely understood. The nontrivial zeros live in the critical strip. The Riemann Hypothesis says every one of them has real part 1/2. That remains open.

What is known: infinitely many nontrivial zeros, counted by the Riemann–von Mangoldt formula; a Vinogradov–Korobov neighbourhood of Re(s) = 1 contains none; Hardy, Selberg, Levinson, Conrey and Pratt–Robles–Zaharescu–Zeindler put a rising positive proportion on the critical line, currently more than five twelfths in the journal literature; Platt and Trudgian verified every zero up to height 3 · 10^{12}. An August 2026 announcement claims 67.2 percent; it is recorded here and not locked. Even a density-one statement would not be RH.

Density is not isolation. Positive-power zero-density estimates at moderate sigma still block polylog StripDens. Finite-height verification does not give Iso_H. The five analytic obligations of the debt-argument remain open. Residual integers do not locate zeros. The Riemann Hypothesis remains open.

*Per aspera ad astra.*
