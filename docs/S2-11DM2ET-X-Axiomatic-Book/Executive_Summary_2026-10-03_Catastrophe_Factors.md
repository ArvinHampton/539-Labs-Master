# Executive Summary: microtubule catastrophe factors, 2026-10-03

Two meanings. Catastrophe is the switch from growth to shortening. A catastrophe factor is a protein that raises that rate.

Walker 1988: frequency falls as tubulin rises; imaging interval 33 ms. The 33 ms frame is not the 25 ms moment. Factor 1.32 refused. MCAK, Kip3, and stathmin raise the switch rate. They do not set 18 fs, the 137 nm rod, or 539.

Not a reopening. No new residual-flux object.
