## Named barrier: ZEFB

**Zero-Entangled Frequency Barrier.**  
The frequency \(\gamma_\star\) is not a free Diophantine parameter — it is a zero ordinate. Generic equidistribution / metric theorems for almost-all \(\gamma\) do **not** transfer to zero ordinates. Euler-product identities for \(\sum p^{-s}\) are singular at zeros of \(\zeta\).
