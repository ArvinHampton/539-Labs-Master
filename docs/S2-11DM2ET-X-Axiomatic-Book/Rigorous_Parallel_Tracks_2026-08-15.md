## TRACK 1 — Physical and model narrative (rigorous refinement as narrative only)

**Label:** Proposed physical mechanism inside the Model. Not a mathematical demonstration that every nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function has real part one half.

### Narrative mechanism (tightened)

The Model narrates the nontrivial zeros as spectral lines forced onto a critical line by five linked elements:

1. Dual continuum sectors supply two sides of a spectral interface. The critical line is treated in the narrative as that interface.
2. Spectral density of the dual-brane continuum organises the allowed frequencies on and near the interface. Contributions away from the interface are said to be suppressed by the density structure itself.
3. Phase locking realises an alternating structure of eta type. In the narrative this locking is the mechanism that pins spectral lines to the interface and prevents drift off the line.
4. Local spacing between successive lines is constrained by Higgs-echo resonances. The narrative uses these resonances only for approximate gap structure, not for a numerical zero table.
5. Phonon-style suppressions remove residual off-line weight in the narrative picture.

Taken together the story asserts: dual spectral density, phase locking, echo spacing, and phonon suppression force the nontrivial zeros onto the critical line.

### What Track 1 rigorously is

- A refined statement of the dual-universe spectral sketch already recorded as heuristic and exploratory
- A proposed physical picture available for internal consistency checks against other continuum notes
- Motivational language for attention to spectral density and phase locking

### What Track 1 rigorously is not

- Not a classical proof that every nontrivial zero has real part one half
- Not a substitute for zero-density estimates, isolation technology, path continuation, or explicit-formula majorants
- Not a licence to place residual packaging numbers or model constants inside a theorem about the locations of the zeros of zeta
- Not a claim that acceptance of the Model by academia would itself prove the Riemann Hypothesis

### Parallel status of Track 1

Open for further narrative refinement. Independent of Track 2. Success or failure of classical estimates does not rewrite the narrative label.

## TRACK 2 — Pure analytic work (rigorous restatement in classical language)

**Label:** Mathematical programme on the locations of the nontrivial zeros of zeta. Success criterion is classical.
**Language rule:** A theorem about zero locations may mention only the zeta function, its Euler product, its zeros, and classical number-theoretic auxiliaries.

### Obligation 1 — Effective density at moderate real part

**Content:** Bound the number N(σ, T) of zeros with real part at least σ and height at most T, for σ moderately above one half, strongly enough to open a usable joint numerical window under present majorant constants.

**Present classical status (literature check 2026-08-15):**
- Ingham-shape exponents remain positive powers of T at moderate abscissae.
- Explicit form of Ingham (Chourasiya–Simonič, arXiv:2507.15184) improves the logarithmic factor but retains the positive power of T.
- Guth–Maynard improves the density exponent in a key mid-range region (A(σ) ≤ 15/(3+5σ) in the relevant interval) but does not reach a polylogarithmic vertical count at moderate σ.
- Near σ → 1, Bellotti-type results and related work drive the exponent toward zero; that region is not moderate real part for the density lever of this programme.
- Systematic exponent-pair optimisations (ANTEDB / related 2025 work) improve some A(σ) values; none tabulated as polylog N_vert at moderate σ.

**Blockage:** Joint numerical window under the majorant constants presently on the books remains empty because classical M(T) is still a positive power of T at moderate σ.

**Next classical lever:** A genuine polylog (or near-polylog) density estimate at some fixed moderate abscissa, or a reduction of the leading majorant constant large enough to reopen a joint window. No such estimate is claimed here.

### Obligation 2 — Isolation at unbounded height

**Content:** Control that, at a fixed rightmost abscissa β⋆ > 1/2, the only zeros on the vertical line Re = β⋆ are a zero and its conjugate (up to explicit-formula truncation), as height tends to infinity.

**Present classical status:**
- Finite-height isolation and verification of zeros on the critical line exist to large explicit heights (computational isolation algorithms; independent verification of RH to those heights).
- Finite-height isolation on a compact vertical segment is trivial because zeros are isolated points.
- There is still no classical theorem that bounds the number of distinct ordinates on a fixed vertical line Re = β⋆ > 1/2 as height tends to infinity.
- Classical zero-density controls bulk counts to the right of σ; it does not force isolation on a single vertical line at unbounded height.
- Multiplicity bounds control multiplicity at a single point, not the number of distinct ordinates on the line.

**Blockage:** Classical ceiling on unbounded Iso_H remains in force.

**Next classical lever:** A theorem that the number of zeros on Re = β⋆ is O(1) or O(polylog T) for some fixed β⋆ > 1/2, or a usable weakening (thin-strip density about β⋆) that feeds residual estimates without full isolation. None claimed here.

### Obligation 3 — Path continuation from on-line phase data

**Content:** Passage from phase information on or near the critical line to global control of the target functional.

**Present status:** Open. No completed path is recorded.

**Next classical lever:** Construction of a continuous or discrete path of estimates that begins from admissible on-line phase data and reaches the required global bound, without circular appeal to RH.

### Obligation 4 — Resonance control off the line

**Content:** Bounds that limit resonant contribution away from the critical line under independently stated classical hypotheses.

**Present status:** Conditional templates exist under named hypotheses. Unconditional control remains open.

**Next classical lever:** Unconditional resonance bounds, or conditional packages with the hypotheses kept fully visible and independent of RH.

### Obligation 5 — Primary target-lemma arms

**Content:** The conjunction of arms that would close the debt argument of this programme.

**Present status:** Not closed. The debt argument remains open.

**Next classical lever:** Closure of the individual arms under classical estimates; only then can the conjunction be examined.
