4. Lever 4. Phase-oriented mollifier

Sought.
An explicit family M of s and X whose design criterion is the phase of P_X or of A_X, not a moment of absolute zeta.

What was written this pass, then tested as a feed.

Candidate family M_phase of (s, X) equals P_X(s) over the modulus of P_X(s), where P_X is the Gonek-Hughes-Keating partial Euler product up to X. The design criterion is exactly arg P_X. The family is explicit because P_X is a finite product over primes.

On the critical line this family restates the already-proved typical on-line Omega. It does not produce the strong log-log lower bound at a maximal-abscissa zero.

Off the line, modulus of P_X is not 1 and arg P_X is not Im log zeta. Transfer onto a zero of maximal real part meets O4.1 through O4.4. Writing the family does not remove those obstacles.

Public mollifiers tested against the design criterion and refused:
Levinson-Conrey-Pratt mollifiers. Design criterion is a moment of absolute zeta times a Dirichlet polynomial, to force zeros onto the line.
Lin arXiv 2508.11108 short mollifiers. Design criterion is a variational combination of derivatives for Levinson's method. Same class.
Cech-Matomaki optimality of mollifiers. Optimality inside the absolute-moment class.
Named bump f-star and M1.4 log-average in Y. Already classified 5 September as not phase-oriented.

Decision on lever 4.
The obvious phase family can be named. It does not feed O-TL. O-Moll stays OPEN. Dropping O-Moll would not close O-TL.
