## What a bridge would be

A map from the written lock integers to a tubulin structural count, with the count on both sides the same kind of object. Packaging counts: 3, 7, 8, 11, 18, 61, 80, 243, 521, 539, 616, 4880. Tubulin counts: 13 or 14 protofilaments, 8 nm repeat as a length, alpha about 451 residues, beta about 445, three domains, two nucleotides.

## Hits and misses

3 and 8 occur on both lists. Three domains is not three generations. The 8 nm repeat is a length, not the packaging integer 8. The distinctive tubulin integer, 13 protofilaments, is not in the lock set. 4880/13 = 375.38. 539/13 = 41.46. 243/13 = 18.69. None is an integer. 451 and 445 are not packaging counts. 539 - 451 = 88, not a lock integer used as a residue count.

## Not a bridge

A shared 3 and a shared 8 are name collisions. No written integer selects 13. No written integer is a residue count of alpha or beta. The sixth stress stays unadopted. Finding a bridge would mean a new map that sends a lock object to 13, or to the residue lists, without inserting the target. That map is not in the written data.
