## 1. Definition restated

(Iso_H) at a fixed rightmost abscissa β⋆ > 1/2 for a zero ρ⋆ = β⋆ + iγ⋆ of multiplicity m ≥ 1:

Up to explicit-formula truncation, the only zeros with Re ρ = β⋆ are {ρ⋆, conjugate ρ⋆}.

---

## 2. Classical ceiling (locked)

From the companion note:

- Zeros are isolated points, so finite-height Iso_H on any compact vertical segment is trivial.
- The functional equation forces only the conjugate as same-abscissa partner for a rightmost zero; the reflected zeros sit strictly left of 1/2.
- Multiplicity bounds control m at a single point, not the number of distinct ordinates on the line Re = β⋆.
- Classical zero-density N(σ,T) controls bulk counts to the right of σ but does not force “at most two zeros on any single vertical line Re = β⋆ > 1/2”.

There is still no classical theorem that bounds the number of distinct ordinates on a fixed vertical line Re = β⋆ > 1/2 as height tends to infinity.

---

## 3. What would constitute progress

Any of the following would advance Direction 2 without claiming Iso_H itself:

- A theorem that the number of zeros on Re = β⋆ is O(1) or O(polylog T) for some fixed β⋆ > 1/2.
- A usable weakening such as StripDens (density bound in a thin vertical strip about β⋆) that feeds residual estimates without full isolation.
- An effective finite-height Iso_H with explicit constants usable inside the ND1 majorant architecture.

None of these are currently available as unconditional classical theorems at moderate abscissae.
