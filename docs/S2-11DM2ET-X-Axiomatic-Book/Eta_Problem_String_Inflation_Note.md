The η-Problem in String Inflation – Detailed Note
Category B exploratory continuum note. Residual discrete algebra remains untouched and Category A. Residual-flux provenance mandatory.

The η-problem is the generic difficulty of keeping the second slow-roll parameter η much smaller than unity. In Planck units η is essentially the second derivative of the potential divided by the potential itself. When |η| is of order one the potential is too curved for the required number of e-folds.

In supergravity and string-derived effective theories the vacuum energy that drives inflation is typically of order the Hubble scale H. Corrections from moduli stabilization then generate a mass term for the inflaton of the same order, producing η ∼ O(1). This occurs for both F-term and (after threshold corrections) D-term constructions.

In warped-brane models the same volume-modulus corrections reappear once the Kähler moduli are stabilized, steepening the otherwise flattened Coulomb potential and limiting the number of e-folds.

Proposed mitigations include fine-tuning of flux parameters, shift symmetries, non-canonical kinetic terms (DBI), multi-field trajectory turns, and purely perturbative stabilization schemes. None of these continuum mechanisms alter the residual discrete core, the 4880-unit flux budget, or the 539-step HQCC result.
