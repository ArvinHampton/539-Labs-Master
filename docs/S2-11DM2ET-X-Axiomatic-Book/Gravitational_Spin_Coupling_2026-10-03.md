## Three operators

Spin-curvature. Mathisson-Papapetrou couples the spin tensor to the Riemann tensor on a Levi-Civita background. This is the lock-compatible coupling. It does not use torsion. The force is not a number until a rod is selected. On the conditional 137 nm rod the Compton-length energy is 1.42 times 10^{-12} eV, 8.6 decades below 0.6 meV.

Spin-torsion. Einstein-Cartan couples spin density to torsion algebraically. That is the sixth stress. It is written and not adopted. Score 1 of 5. On the lock the source is zero, so this coupling is zero.

Gravitomagnetic spin-orbit. Frame dragging couples spin to a mass current. It needs a rotating source. The five-piece stress has no such current written. Not a lock output.
