## Public dimer

The structural subunit is an alpha-beta heterodimer. Nogales, Wolf, and Downing, Nature 391, 199 (1998), fitted an atomic model to a 3.7 angstrom map. Lowe, Li, Downing, and Nogales, Journal of Molecular Biology 313, 1045 (2001), refined it to 3.5 angstrom, PDB 1JFF. The model includes alpha residues 2-34 and 61-439, beta 2-437, one GTP, one GDP, and taxol.

Each monomer is a compact core of two beta-sheets surrounded by helices, in three domains: N-terminal nucleotide site, intermediate domain, and C-terminal motor surface. Alpha and beta share about 40 percent sequence identity. The GTP on alpha is non-exchangeable. The nucleotide on beta is exchangeable.
