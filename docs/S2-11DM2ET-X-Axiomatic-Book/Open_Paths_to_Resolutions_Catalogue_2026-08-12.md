## 2. Five analytic obligations (RH pure Cat A track)

All five remain open. Infrastructure and status notes do not discharge them.

### 2.1 O-M1.2 — Uniform M1.2 under classical zero-density alone

**Architecture:** accepted.  
**Open part:** numerical majorants (A, B, C) that make the uniform statement effective on a concrete height band while using only classical density theorems.

**Resolution criterion:** Explicit constants (A, B, C) derived from a named classical density theorem (Ingham, Chourasiya–Simonič, Guth–Maynard or later) such that the M1.2 remainder architecture holds uniformly on that band. The constants must not rely on residual algebra or model numbers.

### 2.2 O-M1.3bis — Path design that accumulates |Δθ| ≫ log log X

**Status:** open. Monodromy of a zero-free path about a zero has been withdrawn as an engine.

**Resolution criterion:** An explicit path (horizontal or mildly inclined) from the critical line to a maximal abscissa together with a majorant Φ such that the integral of Φ is strictly smaller than half the on-line reservoir on a positive-density set of heights, thereby preserving a large phase increment. The majorant may use only truncated explicit formulae, classical density, and controlled hybrid remainders.

### 2.3 O-PC — Conversion of pair-correlation / n-level density to strong phase lower bounds at O-TL scale

**On-line fact already accepted:** typical Ω ≫ √(log log X).  
**Open part:** strong / offline conversion that reaches the O-TL scale.

**Resolution criterion:** A theorem that upgrades the accepted on-line Omega to a lower bound of size ≫ m log log X (or the precise O-TL target) on a positive-density set, either on the critical line or after controlled continuation, using only pair-correlation, n-level density, or equivalent classical input.

### 2.4 O-Moll — Genuinely phase-oriented mollifier

**Status:** open.

**Resolution criterion:** Construction of a mollifier whose main contribution is to the argument / phase of the relevant hybrid or explicit-formula object rather than to the absolute value, together with error terms that remain smaller than the target phase lower bound. The mollifier must be admissible under ZLA.

### 2.5 O-TL — Target lemma (primary)

**Statement:** |A_X(Y, γ)| ≥ c m log log X_n at maximal abscissa, for a positive-density set of ordinates and with c > 0 absolute.

**Status:** primary open obligation.

**Resolution criterion:** Proof of the displayed lower bound (or an equivalent form that feeds the existing ND1 / B_θ implication chain) under hypotheses that are either unconditional or explicitly named and weaker than RH itself. Residual packaging integers and continuum model constants are forbidden inside the proof.

## 3. Solid directions and their resolution criteria

### 3.1 Direction 1 — Effective density at moderate σ

**Barrier:** classical N(β⋆, T) still carries a positive power of T for every tabulated β⋆ ∈ [0.55, 0.80].

**Resolution criterion:** A theorem of the form N(β⋆, T) = O((log T)^C) for some fixed β⋆ ∈ (1/2, 4/5] and some absolute C, proved with technology admissible under ZLA. Such a theorem would feed the already-proved implication (RM) + polylog StripDens ⇒ Mass-with-A.

### 3.2 Direction 2 — Iso_H technology

**Classical ceiling:** finite-height isolation is trivial; no classical theorem bounds the number of distinct ordinates on the line Re = β⋆ > 1/2 as height tends to infinity.

**Resolution criterion:** A theorem that the number of zeros on Re = β⋆ is O(1) or O(polylog T) for some fixed β⋆ > 1/2, or a usable weakening (StripDens in a thin vertical strip about β⋆) that still feeds the residual estimates. The proved implication (RM) + (Iso_H) ⇒ B_θ remains one-way only.

### 3.3 Direction 3 — Path continuation from on-line Ω

**Current artefact:** template-level majorant Φ assembled from truncated explicit formula, classical density, and GHK remainder (RH_Dir3_P31_Majorant_Phi_Sketch_2026-08-12.md).

**Resolution criterion:** Demonstration that the integral of Φ from 1/2 to σ_* is smaller than half the on-line reservoir on a positive-density set of heights (or a clear density-conditional version of the same statement). Monodromy is excluded.

### 3.4 Direction 4 — Resonance offline

**Open lemma and obstacles O4.1–O4.4 remain active.**

**Resolution criterion:** Control of the GHK error in a thin strip (O4.1), a positive-density set of Kronecker maximisers that avoid nearby zeros (O4.2), continuous argument transfer with tracked branch errors (O4.3), and the necessary correlation estimates (O4.4), sufficient to push a large on-line imaginary part off the critical line while preserving size ≫ √X / log X.

### 3.5 Direction 5 — Mass-with-A under (RM)

**Locked implications:** (RM) + (Iso_H) ⇒ B_θ and (RM) + polylog StripDens ⇒ Mass-with-A are proved one-way statements only.

**Resolution criterion:** Either an unconditional proof of Mass-with-A under residual-mass hypotheses alone, or a proof of the named density hypothesis DH(β⋆, C) that makes the conditional template unconditional at some moderate β⋆.
