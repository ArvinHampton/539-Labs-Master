## 3. Mapping cone of \(\rho_W\) (cochain level)

### 3.1 Definition

For a map of cochain complexes \(\rho:F\to F_{\le W}\), the **mapping cone** is the complex
\[
\mathrm{Cone}^\bullet(\rho)
=
F_{\le W}^\bullet
\oplus
F^{\bullet+1}
\]
with differential
\[
\boxed{
d_{\mathrm{Cone}}(x,y)
=
\bigl(d_{F_{\le W}}x+\rho(y),\;
-\,d_F y\bigr)
}
\]
(sign conventions vary by author; this is the common cohomological cone so that \(\rho\) appears with \(+\)).

Degree:
\[
\deg(x,y)=\deg x=\deg y-1
\quad\text{when }y\in F^{\bullet+1}.
\]

### 3.2 Low degrees (residual thin case)

Generators of interest sit in degrees 0–2 of \(F\). Symbolically:

| Cone degree \(n\) | Summand |
|-------------------|---------|
| \(n\) | \(F_{\le W}^n\oplus F^{n+1}\) |

**Cocycles in degree 2**
\[
d_{\mathrm{Cone}}(x,y)=0
\iff
\begin{cases}
d_{F_{\le W}}x+\rho(y)=0,\\
d_F y=0.
\end{cases}
\]
So \(y\) is a cocycle in \(F^3\) — and thin residual \(F\) has **no independent degree-3 generators** in the locked generator set \(\Rightarrow\) \(y=0\) in the thin model, and \(x\) is a cocycle in \(F_{\le W}^2\).

For the **interesting** comparison of the permanent 2-class, use the **shifted** reading via the standard triangle (next section), or the cone of \(\rho\) placed so that \(H^\bullet(\mathrm{Cone})\) fits the LES.

### 3.3 Standard triangle

There is a distinguished triangle in the derived category
\[
F
\xrightarrow{\rho}
F_{\le W}
\longrightarrow
\mathrm{Cone}(\rho)
\xrightarrow{+1}
\]
inducing the long exact sequence
\[
\cdots
\to
H^n(F)
\xrightarrow{r_W}
H^n(F_{\le W})
\to
H^n(\mathrm{Cone}\,\rho)
\to
H^{n+1}(F)
\to
\cdots
\]

For thin residual form theory with
\[
H^0(F)\cong\mathbb{Q},\ 
H^1(F)=0,\ 
H^2(F)\cong\mathbb{Q}\cdot[\alpha\otimes\delta f],
\]
and the same pattern on \(F_{\le W}\) when \(r_W\neq 0\) (shell still rank-1 in degree 2), the LES around degree 2 collapses to a short exact fragment:

\[
0
\to
H^2(F)
\xrightarrow{r_W}
H^2(F_{\le W})
\to
H^2(\mathrm{Cone}\,\rho)
\to
H^3(F)
\to
\cdots
\]

With thin \(H^3(F)=0\) and \(r_W\) an isomorphism of 1-dimensional spaces (both generated by the permanent class when \(M_{\mathrm{win}}>0\)):

\[
\boxed{
H^2(\mathrm{Cone}\,\rho)=0
\quad\text{in the thin rank-1 model with }r_W\text{ iso}.
}
\]

**Important nuance:**  
\(r_W\) iso on \(H^2\) means **no cohomological obstruction in the cone at degree 2**.  
Tower mass \(M_{\mathrm{tow}}>0\) is **not** a second cohomology class in thin \(F\); it is **extra support of the same class**. The cone does not invent a second permanent form class from tower mass alone.

## 4. What the cone *does* measure in residual practice

Even when \(H^2(\mathrm{Cone}\,\rho)=0\), the cone is useful at **cochain** and **relative** levels.

### 4.1 Relative cochain complex (pair)

Standard identification (up to shift conventions):
\[
\mathrm{Cone}(\rho)
\;\simeq\;
F^\bullet / F_{\le W}^\bullet
\quad\text{or relative cochains }
C^\bullet(F,F_{\le W})
\]
in the sense of forms that **vanish on the shell** (tower-supported cochains).

**Tower-supported representative of the same class**

Take the permanent cocycle \(\beta=\alpha\otimes\delta f\). Split by edges:
\[
\beta=\beta_{\mathrm{win}}+\beta_{\mathrm{tow}},
\qquad
\beta_{\mathrm{win}}=\beta|_{I_{\mathrm{win}}},
\quad
\beta_{\mathrm{tow}}=\beta|_{I_{\mathrm{tow}}}.
\]

Then
\[
\rho(\beta)=\beta_{\mathrm{win}},
\quad
\beta_{\mathrm{tow}}
\text{ is a cochain on the full complex with }
\rho(\beta_{\mathrm{tow}})=0.
\]

If \(d\beta=0\), then
\[
d\beta_{\mathrm{tow}}=-d\beta_{\mathrm{win}},
\]
so \(\beta_{\mathrm{tow}}\) is **not** automatically a cocycle unless \(d\beta_{\mathrm{win}}=0\) in the full complex (boundary terms at the cut edge \(i=W-1\)).

**Cut edge** \(e_{W-1}=(W-1\to W)\) is the interface.  
Relative/tower bookkeeping must include **coboundary leakage across the cut**.

### 4.2 Cone class of the cut

Schematic cocycle in the cone at the cut:
\[
(x,\,y)
=
\bigl(-\rho(\gamma),\, \beta_{\mathrm{tow}}\bigr)
\quad\text{with }
d\gamma=\beta_{\mathrm{win}}
\text{ on shell if exact there, etc.}
\]

In thin residual practice, the constructive diagnostic is simpler:

| Object | Residual meaning |
|--------|------------------|
| \(r_W([\beta])\) | shell visibility of permanent class |
| \(M_{\mathrm{tow}}\) | tower \(L^1\) mass of same class |
| Cone / relative | **support filtration**, not a second \(H^2\) summand |

### 4.3 When would \(H^\bullet(\mathrm{Cone})\) be nontrivial?

| Situation | Cone effect |
|-----------|-------------|
| \(r_W=0\) but \(H^2(F)\neq 0\) | permanent class pure tower ⇒ class maps to nontrivial relative/cone class |
| \(r_W\) not surjective | shell has extra classes not from full \(F\) |
| Thicker \(F\) with \(H^3\neq 0\) | LES allows nontrivial cone in degree 2 |
| Default residual thin \(F\), \(r_{18}\neq 0\) iso | \(H^2(\mathrm{Cone})\cong 0\) |

**Executed default:** cone is **cohomologically quiet in degree 2**; its value is **structural** (exact triangle + support split), not a new Betti number.

## 5. Mapping cone of \(r_W\) on cohomology (vector-space level)

If one forms the cone of the **map of graded vector spaces** \(r_W:H^\bullet(F)\to H^\bullet(F_{\le W})\) with zero differentials on each side:

\[
\mathrm{Cone}^n(r_W)
=
H^n(F_{\le W})\oplus H^{n+1}(F),
\quad
d(x,h)=(r_W(h),\,0)
\]
(with appropriate placement).

Then
\[
H^n(\mathrm{Cone}\,r_W)
\cong
\operatorname{coker}(r_W)_{n-1}
\oplus
\operatorname{ker}(r_W)_n
\]
(up to standard shift bookkeeping).

**Degree 2, residual thin, \(r_W\) iso \(\mathbb{Q}\to\mathbb{Q}\):**
\[
\ker r_W=0,\quad \operatorname{coker} r_W=0
\quad\Rightarrow\quad
\text{trivial cone cohomology in the relevant slot}.
\]

**If \(r_W=0\)** (pure tower class):
\[
\ker r_W=\mathbb{Q},\quad
\operatorname{coker} r_W = H^2(F_{\le W}),
\]
cone measures both “invisible on shell” and “shell’s own classes.”
