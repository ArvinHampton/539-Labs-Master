# Three-Body Observational Alignment

**Date:** 2026-08-01  
**Scope:** LIGO ring-down echoes, Sgr A* stellar dynamics, EHT M87 jet base

## LIGO Ring-Down and Echo Searches

GW190521 remains an outlier: short duration, absent clear inspiral, total mass near 150 solar masses. Re-analyses in 2025 explore the possibility that the signal is an isolated gravitational-wave echo emerging from a wormhole remnant. Network signal-to-noise ratios for the wormhole-echo template are comparable to the standard binary-black-hole interpretation; Bayesian evidence slightly prefers the conventional merger but does not exclude the echo hypothesis. Across the broader LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA catalogue, population searches for stimulated Hawking-radiation echoes or other post-merger echoes yield no statistically robust detections. Standard quasi-normal-mode ring-downs dominate.

## Sgr A* Three-Body Dynamics

ERIS and GRAVITY observations through 2025 confirm that the S-cluster stars and the dusty binary D9 remain on stable orbits despite periapses as close as 100 AU. The environment is less disruptive than earlier analytic estimates suggested. Three-body scattering (Hills mechanism and encounters with intermediate-mass perturbers) accounts for the observed eccentricity distribution and random orbital orientations. A past recoil event following a black-hole merger is consistent with the present configuration. No cascading ejections or infinite-velocity captures are observed.

## EHT M87 Jet Analysis

Multi-epoch 230 GHz polarimetry (2017, 2018, 2021) records a complete reversal of the linear-polarization spiral around the ring. Intermediate-length baselines introduced in 2021 recover a faint Gaussian component offset approximately 320 micro-arcseconds in right ascension and 60 micro-arcseconds in declination, interpreted as the first direct detection of the jet base on horizon scales. The component is aligned with the large-scale jet direction and with the southern arm seen at 86 GHz. The persistent asymmetric ring (median diameter approximately 43 micro-arcseconds) remains connected to a collimated, edge-brightened outflow. Magnetic-field variability is turbulent yet does not destroy large-scale collimation.

## Synthesis

Across gravitational-wave ring-downs, Galactic-centre stellar orbits and horizon-scale jet launching, the data exhibit bounded, coherent evolution. Classical three-body divergences that would produce ejections, unresolved singularities or jet fragmentation are absent. The non-local damping and snap-back mechanisms that guarantee global smoothness are therefore consistent with every currently available high-resolution data set.

Future LIGO O5 runs and expanded EHT arrays (including the Africa Millimetre Telescope) will supply tighter constraints on modulated ring-down precursors and on jet-base morphology.
