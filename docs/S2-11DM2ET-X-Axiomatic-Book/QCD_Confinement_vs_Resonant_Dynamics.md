# QCD confinement vs resonant ternary dynamics

## 1. Confinement in QCD (standard physics)

**Confinement** in QCD is the empirical fact that quarks and gluons are never observed as free asymptotic particles. The asymptotic states of the theory are **colour-singlet hadrons**.

### Static potential and string tension

- The interaction between a static quark–antiquark pair grows **linearly** with separation at large distance.
- The constant of proportionality is the **string tension**, of order **\(\sim 1\,\mathrm{GeV/fm}\)**.
- When separation becomes large enough the string **breaks** by creation of a new quark–antiquark pair, producing two colour-singlet mesons rather than free quarks.

### Lattice QCD evidence

Lattice QCD supplies the strongest theoretical evidence:

| Observable | Role |
|------------|------|
| Wilson loops on large Euclidean lattices | Area law at intermediate distances |
| Extracted string tension | Scales toward continuum; matches phenomenology |
| Static potential, flux-tube profiles | Consistent with confinement |
| Glueball / hybrid spectra | Consistent with confinement |
| Free quarks at \(T=0\), low density | Never produced in practical simulations |

### Analytic status

A complete **analytic proof** of confinement from the continuum QCD Lagrangian is still **lacking**.

- Related Millennium Prize Problem (Clay): **Yang–Mills existence and mass gap** in four-dimensional continuum non-abelian gauge theory.
- Confinement / mass gap have **not** been established rigorously in that continuum setting.
- Many partial results and dual-superconductivity scenarios exist; **none** is a final proof.
