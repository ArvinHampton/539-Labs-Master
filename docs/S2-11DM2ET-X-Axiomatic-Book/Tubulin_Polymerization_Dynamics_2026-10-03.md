## Public process

An alpha-beta dimer, about 8 nm, adds to a protofilament end if it carries GTP. After GTP hydrolysis the lattice is less stable. The microtubule switches between growth and rapid shortening. Catastrophe is the switch to shortening. Rescue is the switch back. Walker et al., Journal of Cell Biology 1988, plus end: association 8.9 per micromolar per second, dissociation during growth 44 per second, rapid shortening 733 dimers per second. Critical concentration near 5 micromolar, where association balances dissociation.

## Times

At 10 micromolar the net plus-end rate is 45 dimers per second, 360 nm/s, about 22 ms per dimer. At 25 micromolar the net rate is 179 dimers per second, about 5.6 ms per dimer. Rapid shortening is 733 dimers per second, 1.36 ms per dimer, 5.86 micrometres per second.

The 137 nm overlay is 17.1 dimer lengths. At the 10 micromolar net rate that length takes 0.38 s to grow. At the rapid-shortening rate it takes 23 ms to disappear. The corrected flow length 5.40 um is 675 dimers and takes 0.92 s to shorten at that rate.
