# Structure of the 42-dim function span V inside the 72-dim label representation W (2026-10-18)
Scripts and outputs are in 2026-10-18-V42-Structure/: v42.py/.txt, cusp6.py/.txt, relations.py/.txt, family.py. They reuse the cusp-part data (uvec.py, fitF.pkl). Local only; nothing pushed.

## 1. Decomposition into irreducibles (Cat A unless marked)
S and T are unitary (≤7e-16), so W = V ⊕ V^⊥ with V^⊥ invariant (8e-16, 1e-15). The commutant dimensions are 1 for V_Eis, 1 for V_cusp, 2 for V^⊥ and 2 for V. So W splits multiplicity-free into 4 pairwise inequivalent irreducibles:

| piece | dim | tr S (n₊/n₋) | mod-4 T exps (/4) | mod-5 (/5) | mod-7 (/7) | local dims |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| V_Eis | 36 | 0 (18/18) | {0,2,3} | {0,1,4} | {0,3,5,6} | 3·3·4 |
| V_cusp | 6 | 0 (3/3) | {3} | {1,4} | {3,5,6} | 1·2·3 |
| V^⊥_a | 12 | 0 (6/6) | {3} | {0,1,4} | {0,3,5,6} | 1·3·4 |
| V^⊥_b | 18 | 0 (9/9) | {0,2,3} | {1,4} | {3,5,6} | 3·2·3 |

In each piece the T-spectrum (simple eigenvalues) is exactly the product of its local exponent sets (Cat A).
Cat B identification: {0,1,4}/{1,4} are the even and odd halves of the Weil representation of the rank-1 form x²/5, of dims (5±1)/2. {0,3,5,6}/{3,5,6} are the even and odd halves for 3x²/7, of dims (7±1)/2. Write A₃ for the mod-4 piece with exponents {0,2,3} and B₁ for the one with {3}. Then

  W ≅ (A₃ ⊕ B₁) ⊗ (X⁺ ⊕ X⁻),  where X^± = W₅^± ⊗ W₇^±,
  V = A₃⊗X⁺ (Eis) ⊕ B₁⊗X⁻ (cusp),  V^⊥ = B₁⊗X⁺ ⊕ A₃⊗X⁻.

So the functions realise the "diagonal" pairing: the mod-4 type is locked to the joint 5/7 parity. The mixed 5/7 parities (W₅^+⊗W₇^− and so on) do not occur in W at all; that is P-invariance. A proof of the identification via SL₂(Z/140) characters was not done.

## 2. Arithmetic: why only 6 cusp combinations (Cat A test, Cat B reading)
- The content is constant on σ-orbits {χ, χ∘σ} (residual 0). This gives 24 characters → 12 forms θ_{c,ε}: c is the σ-orbit representative mod 12 and ε ∈ {0,1} is the infinity type (the characters differ by the class of an element with signs (−,+)).
- **Tie (exact on all 24 cusp labels):** content(ε=1) = −content(ε=0) on positive frequencies and +content(ε=0) on negative frequencies. So θ_{c,0} and θ_{c,1} always occur in one fixed combination Φ_c, which is the c-character restricted to one sign pattern of the norm. That leaves 6 combinations Φ_c (c ∈ {1,3,5,7,9,11} mod 12), and the label × Φ content matrix has full rank 6.
- Reading (Cat B): the ±-cone construction of u fixes the parity at infinity, so the even/odd pair at the two archimedean infinity types is glued. This is an archimedean (sign-of-norm) constraint, not a root-number or Atkin–Lehner effect; root numbers were not tested.
- Each of the 6 T-eigenvectors of V_cusp contains all 12 θ_{c,ε}. T separates frequency classes (B₁⊗X⁻ local data), not Hecke eigenforms.
- Eisenstein part: the genus-character ray-class Eisenstein series from the cusp-part ledger. As a PSL₂(Z) module it is the single irreducible A₃⊗X⁺ (Cat A for irreducibility).

## 3. Geometry and the relations kernel (Cat A)
- 11 labels vanish identically. All have β = −a; their α·70 values are (49,56), (63,42), (7,28), (21,14) and (35, 10k) for k=0..6.
- The other 129 labels fall into exactly **42 proportionality classes**, with u_b = c·u_a for a constant c (the values of c are listed in rel.pkl; I did not check whether they are roots of unity). Class sizes: 3 of size 1, 15 of size 2, 24 of size 4. So V has a monomial basis of 42 distinct functions.
- The kernel (dim 98) is spanned by the 11 zero relations together with 159 pair relations (rank 98).
- Sub-structure: the P-relations (I−P) have rank 68. The improper automorphism R = [[1,7],[0,−1]] (Galois-type, RᵀAR = A, det −1) gives u_{Rα,Rβ} = u_{α,β} exactly (1.6e-15), and its relations have rank 68 and coincide with the P-relations. Adding the zero labels brings the rank to 74. The remaining 24 are extra coincidences inside the size-4 classes, and they cut W (72) down to V (42).

## 4. Involutions and invariant forms
- P: trivial on W (by definition). R: acts as the identity on functions, so all of V lies in R = +1. R does not commute with S or T on the full 140-dim label space (0.3, 1.9) (Cat A).
- Complex conjugation: u_{−α,β} = conj(u_{α,β}) (1e-15). This is an antilinear real structure on V (Cat A for the identity).
- Atkin–Lehner W₄, W₅, W₇, W₃₅: not computed (open).
- Invariant Hermitian forms: on V, a 2-parameter family a⟨·,·⟩_Eis + b⟨·,·⟩_cusp; on W, a 4-parameter family, one positive scalar per irreducible (Cat A, from the commutant dims and unitarity).
- S eigen-splits: tr S = 0 on every irreducible, so n₊ = n₋ = d/2 on each (table) (Cat A).

## 5. The 30-dim complement
V^⊥ = B₁⊗X⁺ ⊕ A₃⊗X⁻ consists of the P-invariant label vectors whose lift is identically zero, i.e. the 30 non-P relations (Cat A). It is not realised by these λ = 1/4 lifts or by their positive-frequency parts (rank 42, previous report). Cat B hypothesis (untested): the mismatched parity pairings correspond to the mixed-type (weight-1 holomorphic-type) ray-class characters, which cancel in u (rev2 §7.2), and would be realised by the corresponding weight-1 or odd lifts.
