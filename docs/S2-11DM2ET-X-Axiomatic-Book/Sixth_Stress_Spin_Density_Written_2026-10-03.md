## The stress

The five-piece stress has no spin density. The sixth piece is the spin density

s_mu nu lambda = (hbar/2) n u_[mu  sigma_nu lambda],

with n the number density of the stipulated nucleon spins and sigma the spin orientation. It is antisymmetric in the last pair. It is not a fifth copy of the energy-momentum tensor. The Cartan equation then reads

T_mu nu lambda = (8 pi G / c^3) s_mu nu lambda.

A quadratic correction to the symmetric stress, of order G s^2, is the contact energy. It is second order in this piece.
