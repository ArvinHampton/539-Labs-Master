# Executive Summary: tubulin polymerization, 2026-10-03

Public kinetics only. Walker 1988 plus end: on-rate 8.9 per micromolar per second, growth off-rate 44 per second, rapid shortening 733 dimers per second. At 10 micromolar, about 22 ms per added dimer. Shortening is 1.36 ms per dimer.

The 137 nm overlay is 17.1 dimers. Growth at 10 micromolar takes 0.38 s. Shortening takes 23 ms. The 23 ms against 25 ms is the public rate times the overlay length, not an identity. 18 fs is 12 decades shorter than a dimer addition. Not a reopening.
