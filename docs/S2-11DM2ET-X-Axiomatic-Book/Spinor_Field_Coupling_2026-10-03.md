# Spinor field coupling

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: exploration. Not an adoption. Not a reopening.
CORE_FREEZE unchanged. No new residual-flux object.

## Three couplings

A Dirac spinor psi couples to the geometry through the spin connection. On the lock the connection is Levi-Civita, so

(i gamma^mu nabla_mu - m) psi = 0,

with nabla the torsion-free covariant derivative. This coupling produces no sixth stress. The five-piece source has no spin density.

An Einstein-Cartan enlargement adds the contorsion. The same equation gains an axial contact proportional to the torsion. That torsion is the sixth stress already written, and only if that stress is adopted. It is not adopted. Score remains 1 of 5.

A Proca coupling, g gamma^mu gamma5 A_mu, is a third operator. The coupling g is unselected. On the lock A = 0, so the term vanishes. The Proca mass does not enter the Dirac operator.

## Source direction

The spinor also sources the spin density, s ~ psi-bar gamma gamma5 psi, up to conventions. That bilinear is the Cartan source only if it is inserted into the sixth stress. Inserting the census is that insertion. It does not follow from the lock equation, because the lock equation has A = 0 and torsion zero.

## Split

Levi-Civita coupling, contorsion coupling, and Proca coupling stay three. Only the first is on the lock. None selects 18 fs, 137 nm, or 539. L1 through L7 are unchanged.
