# Einstein-Hampton equation

## 5D bulk

$$
G_{AB} + \Lambda_5 g_{AB} = 8\pi G_5\, T^{(\mathrm{D2})}_{AB}, \qquad \Lambda_5 = -6k^2
$$

## On +U

$$
{}^{(4)}G_{\mu\nu}
= 8\pi G_4 \Big( T^{(m)}_{\mu\nu} + T^{(r)}_{\mu\nu} + T^{(\mathrm{H})}_{\mu\nu} \Big)
- E_{\mu\nu}
$$

$$
T^{(\mathrm{H})}_{\mu\nu}
= \rho_{\mathrm{DE}}(0)\, W(b)\, u_\mu u_\nu
+ p_{\mathrm{H}}(b)\big( g_{\mu\nu} + u_\mu u_\nu \big)
$$

$$
W(b) = e^{-k(b-b_0)} = \kappa_{\mathrm{dark}}^{b/b_0-1},
\qquad
kb_0 = -\ln\kappa_{\mathrm{dark}},
\qquad
E_{\mu\nu}\big|_{C} = 0
$$

## 00 component

$$
H^2 = \frac{8\pi G_4}{3}\Big( \rho_m + \rho_r + \rho_{\mathrm{DE}}(0)\, W(b) \Big)
$$
