## Dirac coupling

The curved-space Dirac operator replaces the partial derivative with the spin connection. On the lock the connection is Levi-Civita, so the coupling is the usual spin connection of the warped metric. Torsion does not enter.

An electromagnetic minimal coupling, partial_mu to partial_mu plus i e A_mu, is a different substitution. Its 1/c^2 reduction is the ordinary spin-orbit term. The Z = 6 hydrogen-like scale remains 0.026 eV. It is not torsion.

If the connection is enlarged to Einstein-Cartan, the axial contorsion adds a contact term of order hbar c K. For the stipulated hbar/2 density, K = 2.05 times 10^{-44} per metre and the energy is 4.05 times 10^{-51} eV. The lock is not enlarged, so this term is off the lock.

## Mathisson-Papapetrou-Dixon

For a spinning test body on a Levi-Civita background,

D P^mu / D tau = - (1/2) R^mu_nu rho sigma  u^nu S^rho sigma,

D S^mu nu / D tau = P^mu u^nu - P^nu u^mu,

with a supplementary condition. Tulczyjew uses S^mu nu P_nu = 0. Mathisson-Pirani uses S^mu nu u_nu = 0. The standard equation has no torsion. A Riemann-Cartan extension adds a contorsion contraction. On the lock that addition is zero.

The curvature that enters is the lock Riemann, R ~ k^2, with k ell = 0.02531. The rod is unselected, so the force is not a number on the lock.

Conditional overlay only, the 18 fs rod ell = 137 nm: k = 1.85 times 10^5 per metre, k^2 = 3.41 times 10^10 per square metre. With S = hbar/2 and u ~ c, the force scale is 2.70 times 10^{-16} N. Divided by a nucleon mass the formal acceleration is 1.61 times 10^{11} m/s^2. The energy across a nucleon Compton length is 1.42 times 10^{-12} eV, 8.6 decades below 0.6 meV. The large acceleration and the small energy are the same order, read over different lengths. Neither is a lock output. The 137 nm rod is the conditional overlay, not ell_11.
