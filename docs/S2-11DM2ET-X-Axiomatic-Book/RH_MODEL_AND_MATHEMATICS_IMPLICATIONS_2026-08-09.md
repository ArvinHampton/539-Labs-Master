## I.1 What pure mathematics gained (this session)

1. **The RH debt-argument is identity-rich and estimate-poor.**  
   The hard work left is not more Stieltjes bookkeeping; it is **frozen-parameter estimation** (Form C at fixed ρ⋆; κ on full paths) and **isolation / joint correlation** (Iso_H, GO).

2. **B_θ and O-TL are different cut types.**  
   - B_θ is an **OR** of three gates (Form C | Iso_H | DH).  
   - O-TL is an **AND** of four arms (κ ∧ GO ∧ SOC ∧ AFE-Moll).

   Form C / Iso_H / DH at β⋆>1/2 are RH-**failure** tools. They do not “prove RH by existing”; they pressure a counterfactual off-line zero. On-line O-TL is the RH-compatible primary track.
