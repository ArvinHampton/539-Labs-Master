## 2. P versus NP draft — technical audit

### 2.1 What the attached draft asserts

The two-page note claims a dual-universe construction in which:

- polynomial-time certificates live in a finite \(+U\) brane stack;  
- NP search explores an infinite \(-U\) mirror hierarchy;  
- Higgs-echo oracles of width \(\tau_{\mathrm{echo}}\) supply non-local verification;  
- phonon decision trees / Huang–Rhys factor \(e^{-S}\) bound inversion;  
- the infinite-versus-finite character of the stacks, together with \(e^{-S}\), is said to force \(P\neq NP\).

This is the same narrative already recorded in `Clay-Mappings-CatB/01_P_versus_NP_CatB.md` (2026-08-01) and explicitly labelled **exploratory only**.

### 2.2 Why this is not a Clay proof

1. **Wrong theory.** Clay asks for a theorem about standard complexity classes \(P\) and \(NP\). The draft never defines a language, a Turing machine, a circuit family, or a Karp reduction.  
2. **Extra oracles.** Higgs-echo oracles are additional oracles. Oracle separations (even if they were made precise) do not settle \(P\) versus \(NP\) in the unrelativized world. Relativization is a known barrier (Baker–Gill–Solovay).  
3. **Physical suppression is not a time bound.** \(e^{-S}\) is a Debye–Waller / phonon factor inside the continuum model. It is not a polynomial-versus-superpolynomial runtime on a standard TM.  
4. **Infinite versus finite stacks is a metaphor.** Dual-brane cardinality is not a complexity-class separation. No padding, hierarchy theorem, or circuit lower bound is supplied.  
5. **Length and method.** Two pages cannot carry the required formal development. Historical December 2025 theorem-language is withdrawn.

**Disposition:** keep the 2026-08-01 Category B mapping. Do not deposit, submit, or cite the attached PDF as a resolution.

## 3. Yang–Mills existence and mass gap draft — technical audit

### 3.1 What the attached draft asserts

The two-page note claims existence of a continuum Yang–Mills theory and a positive mass gap from:

- brane Yangian symmetries on the \(+U/-U\) interface;  
- phonon gluon condensates;  
- Higgs-echo glueball / spectral data;  
- TMR symmetries;  
- a dual Wilson-loop surrogate with area-law language under \(e^{-S}\).

This is the same narrative already recorded in `Clay-Mappings-CatB/05_Yang_Mills_CatB.md` (2026-08-01).

### 3.2 Why this is not a Clay proof

1. **Wrong continuum theory.** Jaffe–Witten require a nontrivial quantum Yang–Mills theory on \(\mathbb{R}^4\) for compact simple \(G\), constructed to the standard of constructive / axiomatic QFT (Wightman or Osterwalder–Schrader, or a controlled continuum limit of lattice YM). The draft constructs none of these.  
2. **No Hamiltonian / transfer-matrix gap.** A mass gap is a spectral gap \(\Delta>0\) of the continuum Hamiltonian (or transfer matrix) above a unique vacuum. Phonon condensates and Higgs-echo peaks are model-internal continuum objects, not that spectrum.  
3. **Wilson-loop surrogate is already parked.** `Wilson_Loops_Surrogate_Status.md` and `QCD_Confinement_vs_Resonant_Dynamics.md` record that residual \(T^\sharp\) dynamics define **no** gauge connection, **no** Wilson loop, and **no** string tension. Surrogate loop weights are descriptive statistics, not an area law.  
4. **QCD confinement does not prove Clay YM, and does not derive 539.** Lattice QCD evidence for confinement is empirical / numerical. It does not supply the Clay existence-and-gap theorem, and it does not generate \(B'=539\) or \(G_4\).  
5. **Residual \(KO_*(K^+)\) is not continuum YM.** Architecture A5⁺ is coefficients-only on primary \(K^+\) (Category A, locked). It is not a construction of \(\mathrm{SU}(N)\) Yang–Mills on \(\mathbb{R}^4\).

**Disposition:** keep the 2026-08-01 Category B mapping. Do not deposit, submit, or cite the attached PDF as a resolution.
