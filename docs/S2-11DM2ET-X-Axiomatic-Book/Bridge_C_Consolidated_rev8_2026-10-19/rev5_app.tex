\section{Revision 5: errata and changelog (18 October 2026)}\label{app:rev5}
%=====================================================================
Revision~5 adds \S\ref{sec:rev5} and the following corrections. Earlier repository files are not edited; the repository record is
\path{ERRATUM_Consolidated_Rev5_2026-10-18.md}. No claim is made concerning HQCC termination, the twin prime conjecture or the Riemann hypothesis.
\subsection{Errata}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textbf{R5.\arabic*},leftmargin=3.2em]
\item $s=98$: ``$2406\le\dim V\le2408$ (not certified)'' (Theorem~\ref{thm:dims}(iv), \S\ref{sec:open} item~2) is replaced by $\dim V(98)=2406$ (Category~A, Theorem~\ref{thm:s98}). The half-cone bound $2408$ is not sharp, and the gap at $m=7$ is $44$, not $m(m-1)=42$.
\item The rev.~4 Category~B statement ``the gap $m(m-1)$ for all $m$'' is withdrawn. It fails at $m=7$, and further extras are predicted at $m=2k^2-1$ (Category~B, no count claimed for $m=17$).
\item The original orbit lemma (no reduced classes beyond $\pm\nu_0$) is false for some odd $s$ (first at $s=27$). It is replaced by Proposition~\ref{prop:orbit5}. No dimension in the rev.~4 table changes.
\item ``Modulo F1'' is removed from Theorem~\ref{thm:Hs}, Theorem~\ref{thm:C} and Table~\ref{tab:radial}; the expansion is now proved (Proposition~\ref{prop:EM5}). The relevant statements become Category~A, except for any separate Hecke input.
\item The rev.~4 open item ``stable numerics at $x=\tfrac12$ ($s=7,9,10$) and $x=\tfrac13$'' is resolved. The instability came from fitting outside the asymptotic window, and exact closed forms now replace the fits (Propositions~\ref{prop:xhalf}, \ref{prop:xthird}). The rev.~4 instruction not to quote those fits still applies to the old fits.
\item Category~B $\to$ A: $\int_{\Gamma_F}\Xi_1\,dt=0$ and the identification of the weight-$0$ integral with Zwegers' $\Theta_a$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:ct5}). The E-part cocycle, including $C=-\pi/2$ by hand, both signs of $cx+d$, and the $140$-label vector law (Theorem~\ref{thm:coc5}). The ambiguous-class count, the squarefree criterion and the coset property for all $s$ (Theorems~\ref{thm:sqf5}, \ref{thm:coset5}). The vanishing of the $\varepsilon_{\rm all}=-1$ sectors for all odd $s$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:parity5}). $d_2=36$ at $k=6$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:s64}).
\item $u_L$ coverage: $s=32,34,36,40$ are CUSP (Category~A), confirming the rev.~4 prediction.
\end{enumerate}
\subsection{Changelog}
