\subsection{Corrections and supersessions}
\begin{enumerate}[label={R8.\arabic*},leftmargin=3em]
\item \textbf{Scope of K3 statements.} [S39] (\S4, IC-6) treated $N_1=(\Z^2,2A)$ and $N_2$ (and $M_t$) as K3 candidates. The $140$-label representation is $\rho_{N_2}$, not $\rho_{N_1}$ (Theorem~\ref{thm:weil8}); every K3 statement here is for $N_2$ only ([S38], \S6).
\item \textbf{Resolved (Category~B $\to$ A).} [S39]'s hypothesis that the label representation is the Weil representation of an order-$140$ discriminant form (``possibly $\rho_L$ or $\rho_L^*$'') is resolved: $\rho_{\rm rev}\cong\rho_{N_2}\cong\rho_T^*$, with an explicit intertwiner.

\item \textbf{Corpus corrections (Category~B notes).} $H^3(\mathrm{K3};\Z)=0$; $2\cos(2\pi/539.9)$ is algebraic of degree $2699$ as printed.
