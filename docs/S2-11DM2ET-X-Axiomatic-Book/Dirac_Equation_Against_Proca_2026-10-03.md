## Free equation

The free Dirac equation is

(i gamma^mu partial_mu - m) psi = 0.

It has no torsion, no axial field, and no spin-orbit term. Spin-orbit appears only after a Foldy-Wouthuysen reduction of an interaction.

## Coupling written, not derived

A gauge-style coupling to the Proca field A_mu is

(i gamma^mu partial_mu - g gamma^mu gamma5 A_mu - m) psi = 0.

The coupling g is unselected. The axial matrix gamma5 makes this the spin-torsion contact, not the electromagnetic substitution partial_mu to partial_mu + i e A_mu. Those are different operators.

On the lock A = 0, so the extra term vanishes and the equation is the free, or electromagnetically coupled, Dirac equation. The Proca mass mu does not enter the Dirac operator. It enters only the equation for A.

## Reduction

If A is not zero, a Foldy-Wouthuysen reduction of the axial term produces a contact shift of order g hbar c |A|, not the sigma · (E × p) term. For the stipulated census, the near-field scale remains 2.26 times 10^{-39} per metre, and the contact energy remains negligible. The rod and the range are unselected, so this is not a number on the lock.

## Split

Free Dirac, electromagnetic Dirac, and axial Dirac stay three. Only the third uses A. The lock sets A to zero. L1 through L7 are unchanged. The retired identification stands.
