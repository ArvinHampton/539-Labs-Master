# The Proca field

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: exploration. Mass unselected. Not a lock output.
CORE_FREEZE unchanged. Old ingredients stay retired. No new residual-flux object.

## What it is

The Proca field is a massive vector A_mu. It is not the torsion tensor T^lambda_mu nu. It is not the electromagnetic potential. It was written as a Category B carrier for a propagating axial field. The lock does not contain it.

A plane wave has three polarizations when mu is nonzero: two transverse and one longitudinal. The mass term is what allows the longitudinal mode. A massless limit drops that mode and returns a gauge field. That limit is a stipulation.

## Solutions

Homogeneous solution, J = 0: A = 0, or a free wave of mass mu. The lock current is zero, so A = 0 is allowed and is the solution that does not add a sixth stress.

Static solution, point source: A falls as exp(-r/lambda) / r, lambda = hbar / (mu c). The range is unselected. An 8 nm range does not reach 137 nm. The range that matches the census scale is 1.12 times 10^37 m.

## Energy

The field energy is the Proca energy, integral of F^2 plus mu^2 A^2. On the lock solution A = 0 this energy is zero. A nonzero census source would add a positive contact energy. That source is off the lock, and the near-field scale remains 2.26 times 10^{-39} per metre.

## Split

Proca field, electromagnetic potential, and lock torsion stay three. The field does not set L1 through L7. The retired identification stands.
