# Minimum separation for 2 times 10^10 dimers

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: evaluation of the stipulated formula. The value is a fit. Not stipulated. Not a law. Not a reopening.
CORE_FREEZE unchanged. No new residual-flux object.

Formula in use: E_G(1) = G m^2 / a, tau_1 = hbar / E_G(1), N(T) = tau_1 / T, with m = 110 kDa and T = 25 ms. N grows with a. The separation at which N reaches 2 times 10^10 is therefore the minimum separation at which this formula asks for that many dimers.

a_min = N * T * G m^2 / hbar = 1.056 times 10^{-11} m = 10.6 pm.

Any smaller separation needs fewer dimers for a 25 ms moment. Any larger separation needs more. The stipulated 1 fm is smaller than 10.6 pm by a factor 10560 and needs 1.89 times 10^6 dimers. The doubled 2 fm needs 3.79 times 10^6. Both sit below the public count because both sit below this separation.

10.6 pm is not a tubulin diameter, not 1 nm, and not 1 fm. It is the fit already refused. Stating the minimum does not stipulate it. Lock torsion unchanged. Rank 1 and Rank 3 stand.
