# RH Debt Argument — Status (Frozen Rejection as Proof)

**Status:** `RH_OPEN_DEBT_ARGUMENT_NOT_A_PROOF`  
**Verdict:** The phase-debt / leakage argument is **not** a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis.

---

## Where the debt argument fails

### 1. Two objects glued by hand

The model integral \(D(\rho)\) (leakage / debit functional) is essentially **independent of** the zero \(\rho\) once parameters are fixed — it is bounded by model constants.  
The growth of \(\arg P_N\) (or of a chaotic-walk phase) is **asserted**, not derived from that integral.

A contradiction for RH must live **inside one functional** of \(\zeta\) (or an equivalent pure object). Gluing a bounded physical debit to an unproved arithmetic phase growth does not close the logic.

### 2. Model-dependent bound

Constants such as \(\mu=1.55\), \(E_{\mathrm{leak}}\), \(G_4=539.9\), \(\Omega_{\mathrm{DE}}=0.68\), \(\eta_{\mathrm{phonon}}=0.98\) **cannot sit in a theorem about zeros of \(\zeta\)**.  
They are Category B motivation at best.

### 3. Numerical instability of historical debit implementations

Historical \(E_{\mathrm{leak}}\) implementations have already failed their own target scale (order-\(10^1\) vs claimed \(10^{-12}\)).  
A numerically broken debit is not a proof ingredient.

### 4. Lyapunov / chaotic-walk step is not analytic

Claims of the form “\(P_N(\rho)\) performs a chaotic walk with Lyapunov exponent \(\ln 2\)” without a rigorous dynamical system on a defined space do not constitute analysis of \(\zeta\).

### 5. Circular or empty “global debt zero”

“Global debt zero” is either (a) a model postulate or (b) restated RH. It is not an independent theorem.

## What remains salvageable (Cat A only)

The **metaphor** “off-line zeros force large phase of a partial Euler product” can be rephrased as pure conjectures:

- **Conjecture A.** Suitable smoothed phase \(A_X(1/2,t)\) stays controlled on the critical line.
- **Conjecture B.** At a zero with \(\operatorname{Re}\rho\neq 1/2\), \(A_X(\operatorname{Re}\rho,\operatorname{Im}\rho)\) is large (e.g. \(\gg\log\log X\)) for a sequence \(X\to\infty\).

These require L1–L4 (definition, on-line bounds, off-line lower bound from explicit formula / hybrid, no circular RH). L5 is diagnostic numerics only.
