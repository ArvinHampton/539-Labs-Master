# Method Investigation: Half-Isolation (Maynard–Pratt) · Levinson–Ivić Isolation

**Status:** `RH_OPEN_DEBT_ARGUMENT_NOT_A_PROOF`

Half-isolation: local one-sided vertical neighborhood; Dirichlet polynomial + power-sum detection; few such zeros unconditionally; under Hypothesis F ⇒ better density (not a proof of Iso_H).

Levinson–Ivić: horizontal isolation in short rectangles near σ=1 ⇒ better zero-free region and multiplicity bound. Not vertical isolation at arbitrary β⋆.

Neither supplies Iso_H or polylog N_line for general β⋆.
