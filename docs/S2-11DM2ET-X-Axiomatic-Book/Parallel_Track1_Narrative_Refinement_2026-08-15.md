3. Phase locking realises an alternating structure of eta type in the narrative. The locking is what pins spectral lines to the interface rather than allowing them to drift off it.
4. Local spacing constraints come from Higgs-echo resonances in the narrative. These supply the approximate gap structure between successive lines.
5. Phonon-style suppressions remove residual off-line weight in the narrative picture.

Taken together, the story says: dual spectral density plus phase locking plus echo spacing plus phonon suppression force the nontrivial zeros onto the critical line.
