## 0. Verdict

**MATCH: YES (Cat A, exact).** In the label-operator convention Y[i, j(i)], the rev6/rev7 140-label residual representation, taken in its standard normalisation (rem:Sconv) ρ_rev := (S ↦ S_lab⁻¹, T ↦ T_lab), is isomorphic to the Weil representation ρ_L (Borcherds' convention). It is not isomorphic to the dual. Here L is the even lattice

  L = N2 := { x ∈ Z² : x₂ even } with the form A = [[2,7],[7,7]] (the even sublattice of the odd lattice (Z², A)),
  Gram (basis (1,0), (0,2)) = [[2,14],[14,28]], det = −140,
  L ≅ ⟨2⟩ ⊕ ⟨−70⟩ (basis change P = [[1,−7],[0,1]]; Q = 2(x+7y)² − 70y²).

No character twist is needed. No metaplectic sign arises, because sign(L) = 0, so ρ_L is an honest SL₂(Z) representation, with e(sign/8) = 1. An explicit intertwiner is given below and verified exactly in Z[ζ₂₈₀].

## 2. The check (K-2 … K-4)

**Conventions.**
- Borcherds: ρ_L(T)e_γ = e(q(γ))e_γ and ρ_L(S)e_γ = e(−sign/8)|A|^{−1/2} Σ_δ e(−b(γ,δ))e_δ. The dual ρ_L* is the complex conjugate.
- Rev side: the labels are (α, β), with α ∈ ({0,a}+L′)/Z² (70 classes), β ∈ {0, −a}, a = (7/10, −1/5). The operators are built in the Y[i, j(i)] form by subclassing `family_s.Family`, which is imported unmodified, with exact Fraction phases. All denominators divide 140, and the result agrees with the floating construction to 1e-16.

**K-2 (Cat A). T-spectrum.** Rev T is a monomial operator whose label permutation has 70 fixed points and 35 two-cycles. Its eigenvalue multiset (36 distinct values) equals that of ρ_{N2}(T). It does **not** equal ρ_{N2}*(T) or the spectrum of N1 = (Z², 2A) = [[4,14],[14,14]]. Other det −140 even binary lattices tested do not match: 2⟨5,−7⟩, 2⟨−5,7⟩, 2[[3,1],[1,−12]] and their negatives. 2⟨1,−35⟩ and 2[[1,1],[1,−34]] match only because both are ≅ N2. Their negatives match the dual.

**K-3 (Cat A, exact mod ℓ = 840841 ≡ 1 mod 280; superseded by K-4 for the iso itself).**
- (S_lab⁻¹, T) satisfies S⁴ = I and (ST)³ = S², as every Weil representation of signature 0 does.
- (S_lab, T) fails (ST)³ = S², so it is not a representation of SL₂(Z) in this normalisation. This confirms rem:Sconv.
- dim Hom(ρ_{N2}, (S_lab⁻¹, T)) = 8, and a generic element has rank 140. The 8 is consistent with 8 multiplicity-one irreducible sectors W₅^±⊗W₇^±⊗{A₃, B₁}.
- Hom(ρ_{N2}, (S_lab, T)) has dim 4, and every element has rank ≤ 72. The overlap is only on the 72-dimensional subspace W_P, where the two normalisations agree.

**K-4 (Cat A, exact in Z[ζ₂₈₀]). Closed-form intertwiner.** Index ρ_{N2} by z ∈ L′/L, written in Z²-coordinates, and let β₁ denote the stored label of −a (mod Z²). Define

  X e_z = u_{([z], 0)} + e( B(z, β₁) ) · u_{([z], β₁)},  where B(u,v) = uᵀAv and [z] = z mod Z².

Each α class has exactly two lifts z, z + (0,1) in L′/L.

Results:
- X·ρ_{N2}(S) = S_lab⁻¹·X: all 140² entries checked, 0 failures.
- X·ρ_{N2}(T) = T_lab·X: all entries checked, 0 failures.
- Both identities were verified by exact reduction mod Φ₂₈₀.
- X is invertible. Each 2×2 block has determinant e(B(z+(0,1),β₁)) − e(B(z,β₁)) ≠ 0, because B((0,1), β₁) ≡ 1/2 mod 1.

So ρ_rev ≅ ρ_{N2} exactly. (A second exact solution with offsets {0, 4/5} is in `explicit.py`/`X_explicit.pkl`. It is another element of the 8-dimensional Hom space.)

**Dual / twist / metaplectic summary.**
- ρ_rev ≇ ρ_{N2}*, because the T-spectra differ.
- No twist by a character of SL₂(Z) is needed, since X intertwines with the untwisted ρ.
- sign = 0, so there is no metaplectic sign.
- Any even lattice with discriminant form ≅ q_{N2} gives the same representation, e.g. N2 ⊕ U, N2 ⊕ E8, and the T of §3.

## 3. K3 consequences

**K-5 (Cat A, elementary). Explicit primitive embedding and transcendental lattice.**
- Let U⊕U have bases e₁, f₁, e₂, f₂ with e·f = 1. Put n₁ = e₁+f₁ (norm 2) and n₂ = e₂ − 35f₂ (norm −70).
- span(n₁, n₂) ≅ N2. It is primitive (the gcd of its 2×2 minors is 1).
- Its orthogonal complement in U⊕U is spanned by t₁ = e₁ − f₁ (norm −2) and t₂ = e₂ + 35f₂ (norm 70). These are orthogonal to n₁ and n₂, and their Gram determinant is −140 = −disc, so they span the full complement (index 1).
- Hence, inside Λ_K3 = U³ ⊕ E8(−1)² (signature (3,19)):

  T := N2^⊥ = ⟨−2⟩ ⊕ ⟨70⟩ ⊕ U ⊕ E8(−1)², signature (2,18), A_T ≅ Z/2 × Z/70,
  q_T(x,y) = −x²/4 + y²/140 = −q_{N2}(x,y) under the identity identification of generators.

This is consistent with Nikulin Prop. 1.6.1. The glue map is the one induced by the unimodular overlattice U⊕U.

**K-6 (Cat A). The residual law in K3 terms.** sign(T) = −16 ≡ 0 mod 8, so ρ_T(S) and ρ_T(T) are the complex conjugates of ρ_{N2}(S) and ρ_{N2}(T) under the identification above. Hence

  **ρ_rev ≅ ρ_{N2} ≅ ρ_T* = ρ̄_T** (Borcherds convention, honest SL₂(Z)-representations).

Equivalently, rev6's residual law transforms under the dual Weil representation of a signature-(2,18) lattice. That lattice is the transcendental lattice of an N2-polarised K3 surface. The statement is purely lattice-theoretic, so this item is Cat A on its own. The geometric reading in K-7 adds cited theorems.

**K-7 (Cat A (cited)). The K3 exists and the lattices are unique.**
- *Existence of the embedding and of T.* K-5 makes this explicit. Nikulin Cor. 1.12.3 is not needed.
- *Uniqueness of the primitive embedding N2 ↪ Λ_K3 (up to O(Λ_K3)).* Nikulin, "Integral symmetric bilinear forms…" (1979/80), Thm. 1.14.4: rk Λ − rk N2 = 20 ≥ 2 + l(A_{N2}) = 4, and 3 > 1, 19 > 1. So T is unique up to isometry.
- *Existence of a projective K3 X with NS(X) ≅ N2 and T_X ≅ T.* Morrison, "On K3 surfaces with large Picard number", Invent. Math. 75 (1984), Cor. 2.9(i): ρ ≤ 10, so every even lattice of signature (1, ρ−1) is NS of some algebraic K3. This also follows from surjectivity of the period map.
- *Citation status.* The theorem numbers were checked against secondary sources (arXiv 1312.4421, arXiv 2303.03294, and a Springer "coupling pairs" paper), not against the original PDFs.
- N2 contains no (−2)-vectors and no isotropic vectors (IC-5). So X has no smooth rational curves or elliptic fibrations, and the ample cone equals the positive cone component.

**K-8 (Cat A, plus Cat A (cited) for the lift). The Pell automorph γ, corrected to N2.** γ = [[−1,−7],[2,13]] (eigenvalues 6 ± √35, γᵀAγ = A) preserves N2.

Its action on A_{N2}, computed (`closed_exact.py`):

| part | γ̄ |
|---|---|
| 2-part ((Z/2)²) | a non-trivial involution, not ±1 |
| 5-part | +1 |
| 7-part | −1 |

So γ̄ ≠ ±1 and γ̄² = id. This matches rev6's local form Z_(s) ⊗ I ⊗ Z₂ on the 7/5/2 parts.

O(N2) = {±γᵏ, ±rγᵏ} with r = diag(1,−1) in the ⟨2⟩⊕⟨−70⟩ basis. Proper automorphs correspond to norm-1 units of Z[√35], with ε = 6+√35 fundamental, and x² − 35y² = −1 has no solution because −1 is a non-square mod 7.

Table (`aut.py`):

|---|---|---|
| γ^{2j} | +1 | yes |
| rγ^{2j} | −1 | yes |
| odd powers | "other" | — |

Lift via strong Torelli (Burns–Rapoport / Piatetski-Shapiro–Shafarevich; e.g. Huybrechts, *Lectures on K3 Surfaces*, Thm. 7.5.3) together with Nikulin Cor. 1.5.2 gluing:
- γ² ⊕ id_T is a Hodge isometry preserving the ample cone. It is therefore induced by a unique f ∈ Aut(X). f has infinite order, and its entropy is h(f) = log ρ(f*) = log(6+√35)² = 2 log(6+√35) ≈ 4.93. (Spectral radius 71 + 12√35; it is a quadratic unit, not a higher-degree Salem number.)
- r ⊕ (−id_T) is induced by a non-symplectic involution ι.
- For **very general** X in the N2-polarised family, O_Hodge(T_X) = {±1}. Then Aut(X) ≅ ⟨f, ι⟩ ≅ Z ⋊ Z/2 (infinite dihedral), with H⁰-action injective by Torelli.
- **γ itself does not lift** for very general X, because γ̄ ≠ ±1.

## 4. What is now Cat A for K3
1. ρ_rev ≅ ρ_{N2}, exactly, with an explicit closed-form intertwiner (K-2, K-4).
2. ρ_rev ≇ ρ_{N2}*, ρ_{N1}, or ρ_{N1}*. (S_lab, T) is not an SL₂(Z)-representation (K-2, K-3).
3. Explicit T = ⟨−2⟩⊕⟨70⟩⊕U⊕E8(−1)², with signature (2,18) and q_T = −q_{N2}, and ρ_rev ≅ ρ_T* (K-5, K-6).
4. Cat A (cited): existence of an N2-polarised projective K3, uniqueness of the embedding and of T (Nikulin 1.14.4, Morrison Cor. 2.9(i)), and absence of (−2) curves (K-7).
5. γ̄ on A_{N2} is (2: non-trivial involution, 5: +1, 7: −1) and γ̄² = 1 (K-8).
6. Cat A (cited): γ² lifts to an automorphism of entropy 2 log(6+√35). For very general X, Aut(X) ≅ D_∞ = ⟨f, ι⟩ and γ does not lift.

## 5. Still Cat B / open
