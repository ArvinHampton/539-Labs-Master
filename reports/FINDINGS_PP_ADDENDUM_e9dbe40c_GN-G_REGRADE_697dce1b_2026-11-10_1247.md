**4.2 Row-2 rider from L-GN (contraction only; g^yy = 1, g^ya = 0, so g⁻¹ is block-diagonal).**
- R_ab = R^y_ayb + R^c_acb = R_yayb + h^cd R_cadb. By (c), R_yayb = −½∂_y²h_ab + K_acK^c_b. By (a), the Gauss term is ∂_y²h-free. So R_ab = −½∂_y²h_ab + (∂_y²h-free), unit coefficient, in any D.
- R_yy = h^ab R_ayby = h^ab R_yayb. R = R_yy + h^ab R_ab. Hence G_yy = R_yy − ½R = −½ h^ab h^cd R_cadb. This is pure Gauss and ∂_y²h-free by (a).
- G_ya = R_ya = h^bc R_cyba carries exactly one y index. It is Codazzi and ∂_y²h-free by (b).
- Every step is dimension-free. This is the row-2 split in general D.

**4.3 Row-3 forms from the accepted proof's step-1 Christoffels (substitution only).**
