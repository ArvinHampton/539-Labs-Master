## Full text (note_tweet)
New Pure Mathematics discoveries that have Applied Math applications.

What carries over to applied work...

Rigorous local limit theorems. The block step (A56, A59) is a local CLT for lattice sums with explicit constants, checked in ball arithmetic. The same kind of bound is what reliable error estimates need in discrete probability, Monte Carlo on lattices and coding theory. The explicit-constants machinery and the interval-arithmetic pipeline are reusable even though the specific application is q-series.

Certified computation. The workflow is preregistered, exact or interval arithmetic, two independent re-implementations, and a disclosed list of what is not proved. It is the same standard applied numerical analysis uses for verified computing.

Exact modular certificates. The Bridge C certificate (rank 36, exact relations over Z[ζ9800], checked mod four primes) shows how to certify a numerical linear-algebra claim exactly. That is directly transferable to any applied problem where a floating-point rank or relation needs proof.

Galois and discriminant criteria. The pure-even quintics work gives explicit square-discriminant parametrizations and a degree-6 resolvent test. Those are useful wherever polynomial root structure matters, such as algebraic solvers and parameter identifiability.
