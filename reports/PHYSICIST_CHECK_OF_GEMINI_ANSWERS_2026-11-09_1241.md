A1 PARTLY CORRECT. Split and definite are distinct Q-forms. Gemini's local-uniqueness reason is wrong, because quadratic spaces over Q_23 are classified by dimension, discriminant and Hasse invariant. Gamma48 tensor Q_23 is split anyway, since it is unimodular at the odd prime 23.
A2 GEMINI WRONG. Spin_48 is simply connected and Spin_48(Q_23) is noncompact, so G(Q)G(Q_23) is dense in G(A). A form fixed by G(Q_23) is therefore constant and pi is trivial. This stands as a theorem under those hypotheses.
A3 MOSTLY WRONG. |U(140)| = phi(140) = 48 is correct. The trace form is Q(x) = sum a_g a_{g^-1}, with no minus sign. The embeddings of 49a1 and Q(sqrt35) are not constructed.
A4 CORRECT IN OUTLINE (Selmer / Poitou-Tate). Reciprocity does not kill [xi]. Local Tate pairings take values in Q/Z.
A5 UNSUPPORTED. 2*prod(23^i+1) counts maximal isotropic subspaces, not a stabilizer order. OPEN.
A6 NUMEROLOGY. pi/4 = 0.785398 is not 0.7858. OPEN.
B1 CORRECT (Majorana). The physical gloss is speculative.
B2 CORRECT. An anti-diagonal operator on H+ (+) H-, which squares to gamma0 gamma0* on each block.
B3 GEMINI WRONG. -H = -alpha.p - beta m, while m -> -m gives alpha.p - beta m. The spectrum is symmetric, so this gives no negative inertial mass.
B4 A CONVENTION. -Z holds only if the map includes C.
B5 UNVERIFIED. No derivation given.
C1 WRONG REASONING. With S = int sqrt(-g)[R/2k^2 + L_F] the field equation is G = k^2 T. With G = 2k^2 T', T' is half the standard tensor, not double. The normalization mismatch stands.
C2 NOT USABLE. The Freund-Rubin flux needs the volume form of a 4D or 7D factor, the index legs are unclear, and there is no Einstein check. OPEN.
C3 INCONSISTENT. Gemini drops -K_S from G_ext = 6k^2 - K_S, and Delta^2 is not derived. OPEN.
C4 WRONG EXAMPLE. SO(16)xSO(16) is a 10D heterotic string, and bosonic M-theory is conjectural. OPEN.
C5 WRONG. dim SL(6,R)/SO(6) = 20, not 35. OPEN.
C6 WRONG. g^{MN}T^L_{MN} = 0 by antisymmetry. The trace is T_L = T^K_{KL}.
D CONCLUSIONS CORRECT. The K3 reason is wrong. Constant curvature plus Ricci-flat implies flat, and K3 has chi = 24, so it is not flat.
