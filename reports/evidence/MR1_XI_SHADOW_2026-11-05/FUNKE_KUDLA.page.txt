These series are modular modular completions, defined by means of generalized error functions, of certain non-modular holomorphic generating series associated to lattice vectors in positive cones. We show that these modular forms arise as integrals of the theta forms, defined in work of Millson and the second author, over certain singular q-cubes. We also give an explicit formula for the integrals of such forms over singular q-simplices. The sign function occurring in the holomorphic generating series arises as in intersection number of the singular q-cube or q-simplex with a totally geodesic subsymmetric space of codimension q. The cubical case for q=2 was treated in [11].
