[math/0212286] On Two Geometric Theta Lifts Skip to main content Search Submit Donate Log in Search arXiv Press Enter to search · Advanced search Mathematics > Number Theory arXiv:math/0212286 (math) [Submitted on 20 Dec 2002 ( v1 ), last revised 14 Mar 2003 (this version, v4)] Title: On Two Geometric Theta Lifts Authors: Jan Hendrik Bruinier , Jens Funke View a PDF of the paper titled On Two Geometric Theta Lifts, by Jan Hendrik Bruinier and Jens Funke View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: The theta correspondence has been an important tool in studying cycles in locally symmetric spaces of orthogonal type. In this paper, we establish for O(p,2) an adjointness result between Borcherds' singular theta lift and the Kudla-Millson theta lift. We extend this result to arbitrary signature by introducing a new Borcherds lift for O(p,q). On the geometric side, this lift can be interpreted as a differential character in the sense of Cheeger and Simons.
