42.3    Where the shadow Ξ enters
RTA describes a weight- 32 shadow Ξ = Ξeven +Ξodd as “the residual-weighted family of shifted partial
thetas of squares whose outer coeﬀicients are the residual disc-35 series”, and a weight- 21 period
candidate as its Eichler integral. Neither is written as an explicit series with stated coeﬀicients, so
no statement about Ξ can be proved from the written definitions. What is proved here bounds the
place where Ξ must enter:

 (i) at order t−1 the normalized series is governed by V35 with no correction term (Theorem 34.7);

(ii) at order t−1/2 the coeﬀicient A1 is an explicit finite sum of drift, sawtooth and ray terms
                                  √
     (Proposition 35.11) with A1 / π algebraic (Corollary 35.8); any S-covariant completion of A1
     must therefore come from a term that is not visible at order t−1 ;

(iii) at order t0 the coeﬀicient A2 is explicit (Theorem 35.6).

For the 105 companion series, Section 37 determines the shadow structure: the P -odd part has the
ξ1 -image of Theorem 37.13(i), and the P -even part has no weight-one law and the Laplacian defect
of Theorem 37.11. This does not identify Ξ, since Ξ is not written as an explicit series. At order
t−1/2 the coeﬀicient is fully explained by the completion and line terms (Theorem 38.5). Whether
a completion term of Zwegers type, with coeﬀicients derived from the error-function completion
of H7 , makes A1 or A2 S-covariant is open (Problem 1.). The numerical tests carried out so far
(Appendix D) found no closure for any regrouping of the finite sum or for the simplest sawtooth
candidate.
