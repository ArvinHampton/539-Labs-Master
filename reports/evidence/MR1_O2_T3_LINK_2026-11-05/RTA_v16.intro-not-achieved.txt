1     Introduction
1.1   Goal
The aim of this work is to tie Residual Ternary Algebra (RTA) to a foundation: to identify which
RTA objects are governed by modular, mock-modular or vector-valued modular structure, and to
prove each such statement. RTA, as written, has no algebraic axioms. Its objects are (a) elementary
arithmetic of the ternary map T3 and of the residual cardinality 539 (packaging identities, the
equitable partition 539 = 3 · 68 + 5 · 67, the carrier Ores ); (b) the residual series gs ; (c) the Bridge
Series C and its pieces Caxis , Cthird ; and (d) a weight- 32 shadow Ξ and a weight- 12 period-function
candidate, described in words but not defined as explicit series. A “foundation” here means a
theorem placing an object of (a)–(d) inside a structure with known transformation laws under
SL2 (Z).

1.2   What is achieved
(1) For the s-series (Part I): the coeﬀicient expansion of gs , its zero families, sign laws for even s,
    and complete zero sets and residue-class occupancy for odd s ≤ 33. In particular the residue
    law of g7 recorded in RTA follows from the expansion, and its converse holds (Corollary 42.2).

(2) For the Bridge Series (Part II): C and Cthird share the orthant √    remainder P7orth ; up to theta
    and mock pieces this is a partial theta series of one ray class of Q( 35); a Baker-type argument
    excludes every scalar modular or mock residual law, including every scalar W7 slash law; the
    leading radial data carry a vector-valued structure coming from a Siegel theta lift.

(3) For the 35-component law vector (Part III, new): the leading profile of C is I e(7x/20)K20 (x)
    and lies outside V35 ; the normalized series q −7/20 C has profile I K20 ∈ V35 and satisfies the
    vector-valued S-law of V35 ; the radial expansion of q −7/20 C and of 105 companion series is
    computed in closed form through order t0 at every rational point.

(4) For the foundation question (§42): every written link between RTA and the framework above
    is listed with its status.

1.3   What is not achieved
(1) No written object links T3 , Ores , the number 539 or the packaging identities to gs , C or V35 .
    These parts of RTA are not tied to the modular framework by anything proved here.

(2) The shadow Ξ is not identified. The vector-valued law of V35 holds at leading order only; at
    order t−1/2 no law of the same kind is known, and the numerical evidence (Appendix D) is
    against closure inside the companion span.

(3) No link to the gs family is proved beyond the shared definition and the residue law.

(4) Nothing here concerns the twin prime conjecture, the Riemann hypothesis, or any security
    property of a hash construction.

1.5   Organisation
Section 2 fixes common notation and resolves notational conflicts between the parts. Part I (Sec-
tions 3–8) is the s-series. Part II (Sections 9–32) is the Bridge Series. Part III (Sections 33–36) is
the 35-component law vector and the normalized Bridge Series. Section 42 treats the foundation
question and Section 43 lists open problems. Appendices A–C contain the computational records
of Parts I–II, and Appendix D the Category B record of Part III.
