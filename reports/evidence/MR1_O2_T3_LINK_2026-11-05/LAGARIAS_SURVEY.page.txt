Lagarias View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: The 3x+ 1 problem concerns iteration of the map on the integers given by T(n) = (3n+1)/2 if n is odd; T(n) = n/2 if n is even. The 3x+1 Conjecture asserts that for every positive integer n &gt; 1 the forward orbit of n under iteration by T includes the integer 1. This paper is an annotated bibliography of work done on the 3x+1 problem and related problems from 1963 through 1999. At present the 3x+1 Conjecture remains unsolved.
