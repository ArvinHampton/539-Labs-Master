                                                                              ANKUSH GOSWAMI AND ROBERT OSBURN


                                                  Abstract. We explicitly prove the quantum modularity of partial theta series with even or
                                                  odd periodic coefficients. As an application, we show that the Kontsevich-Zagier series Ft (q)
                                                  which matches (at a root of unity) the colored Jones polynomial for the family of torus knots
                                                  T (3, 2t ), t ≥ 2, is a weight 3/2 quantum modular form. This generalizes Zagier’s result on the
                                                  quantum modularity for the “strange” series F (q).
