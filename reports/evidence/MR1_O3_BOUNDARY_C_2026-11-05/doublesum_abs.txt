[2205.02643] Quantum Modular Forms from Real Quadratic Double Sums Skip to main content Search Submit Donate Log in Search arXiv Press Enter to search · Advanced search Mathematics > Number Theory arXiv:2205.02643 (math) [Submitted on 5 May 2022 ( v1 ), last revised 12 Apr 2023 (this version, v3)] Title: Quantum Modular Forms from Real Quadratic Double Sums Authors: Kathrin Bringmann , Caner Nazaroglu View a PDF of the paper titled Quantum Modular Forms from Real Quadratic Double Sums, by Kathrin Bringmann and 1 other authors View PDF HTML (experimental) Abstract: In 2015, Lovejoy and Osburn discovered twelve $q$-hypergeometric series and proved that their Fourier coefficients can be understood as counting functions of ideals in certain quadratic fields. In this paper, we study their modular and quantum modular properties and show that they yield three vector-valued quantum modular forms on the group $\Gamma_0 (2)$.
