Abstract. In this paper, we give a proof of the classical Kronecker
limit formulas using the distribution relation of the Eisenstein-Kronecker
series. Using a similar idea, we then prove p-adic analogues of the Kronecker limit formulas for the p-adic Eisenstein-Kronecker functions defined in our previous paper.
