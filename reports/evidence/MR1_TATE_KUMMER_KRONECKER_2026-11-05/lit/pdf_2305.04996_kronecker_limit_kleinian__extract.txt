A FIRST KRONECKER LIMIT FORMULA FOR KLEINIAN
GROUPS
ZIHAN MIAO, ANH NGUYEN, AND TIAN AN WONG
Abstract. We prove a first Kronecker limit formula for cofinite discrete subgroups of SL(2, C), also called Kleinian groups, generalizing a method of Goldstein over SL(2, R). The proof uses the Fourier expansion of Eisenstein series,
leading to an analogue of the logarithm of the Dedekind eta function, and
whose transformation law produces a function analogous to one related to
Dedekind sums. We derive several basic properties of this new function.

results for imaginary quadratic fields.
