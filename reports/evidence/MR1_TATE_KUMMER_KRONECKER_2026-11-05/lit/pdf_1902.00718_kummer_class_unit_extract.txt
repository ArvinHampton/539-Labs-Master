AN ANALOGUE OF KUMMER’S RELATION BETWEEN
THE IDEAL CLASS NUMBER AND THE UNIT INDEX OF
CYCLOTOMIC FIELDS

arXiv:1902.00718v3 [math.NT] 30 Jul 2019

SU HU, MIN-SOO KIM, AND YAN LI*
Abstract. In this paper, we obtain a formula for the special value of
Euler-Dirichlet L-function LE (s, χ) at s = 1. This leads to another class
number formula of Q(µm )+ , the maximal real subfield of mth cyclotomic
field. From this formula, we construct a new type of cyclotomic units in
Q(µpn ), which implies a similar Kummer’s relation between the ideal
class number of Q(µpn )+ and the unit index.

Kummer’s relation between the class number of Q(µpn )+ and the unit index
(see Theorem 1.8).
