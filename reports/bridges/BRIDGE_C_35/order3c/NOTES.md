# order3c notes (descriptive)
- **Ingredients of A2.** A2 has four kinds of term: a 2-D moment K(l) of the drift, edge-linear B1 terms, B2 terms weighted by the cross coefficient q, and a corner term B1·B1. The corner and B2 terms are elementary (Dedekind-sum-type) finite sums. Only K(l) and Ar involve transcendental integrals.
- **Closure not tested.** The S-closure of A2 was not tested. It is the natural next preregistration: does A2 (or A2 minus a completion term) satisfy a weight-0 vector law?
- **Grid.** The grid was scaled to the denominator and to Q(21,−1) = 595/2, and no post-hoc adjustment was needed.
- **Process.** Cutoff X = 36/t_min (truncation < e^{−36}). Runtime per x was up to ~30 min, single-threaded, under nice.
