# order4b notes (descriptive)
- **Rank of A2.** A2 has rank 73, between the rank of A0 (36) and that of A1 (105). This is consistent with the B1·B1 and B2 finite sums adding new directions on top of the area and drift terms.
- **Pure law.** The doubling of the joint rank at every weight rules out a pure vector law at order t^0 on this grid.
- **Affine law.** The affine test is near saturation. A sharper version needs more rows: more points XS (denominators beyond 15), or the test split by characteristic block (24 + 12 for A0).
- **Process.** Eight point-chunks were run in parallel processes, each single-threaded under nice, each under 2.5 GB.
