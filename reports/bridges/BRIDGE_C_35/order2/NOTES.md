# NOTES (order 2). Times America/Chicago (CDT).
- PREREG.md says "about 8:30 AM"; it was actually written and hashed at about 8:22–8:23 AM (folder created 8:21). The content was unchanged before any run.
- **Map 1 numerics, design error.** I scaled the t-grid with the denominator (t·(2m)² ≤ 0.08), as asked, but missed that the K± pieces have the long edge u1 = (21,−1), Q(u1) = 297.5. The regime parameter is t·Q(u1)·(2m)², which was 0.74 to 24, so the fits at m ∈ {2,3,4,6,8,9} were outside the asymptotic range. m = 1, 5, 7, 10 were partly or fully unaffected because the K± contributions are small or cancel there. Preregistered verdict kept as "falsified". Fixed post hoc with a new script, `posthoc_map1_conescale.py`, using a grid scaled to 297.5·(2m)²: 9 of 9 points agree to ≤ 1e−5. Lesson for future preregistrations: scale t by the largest Q(u) over all cone edges times N².
- **Map 3 prereg, sign error.** B_w was written as |x|^{−w}·A1(−1/x), but the parent law A0(−1/x) = |x|^{−1}M·A0(x) needs the row factor |x|^{+w}. The preregistered control failed (72 ≠ 36), so Map 3 as preregistered is invalid. Both conventions were computed in the same frozen run; the corrected convention passes the control. Reported as a disclosed deviation; Category B only.
