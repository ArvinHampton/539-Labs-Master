# order46 PREREG: exact law for C2b in the 54-dim Bernoulli-column span (decision document; written and hashed before any numerics)
Question: is there a written, non-fitted candidate for an exact law C2b(−1/x) = T·x·C2b(x) (or an x-weighted variant) with T built from L_B / the G S-law / the order43–45 structure?
## Obstruction (stated in advance)
1. order38 RG35: the best ARBITRARY fixed 105×105 matrix T leaves r_B = 0.00206 > 0 on the 216-point grid. So no fixed-matrix law of the form S = x·C2b(x)·T is exact, whatever T is. Any written candidate T (κM, M_b, ΠM̄, …) is bounded below by this floor and cannot be exact. A test of such a candidate is known to fail in advance, so it is vacuous as an "exact law" test.
2. The exact laws available are:
   - L_B (ii): x-dependent, into shifted characteristics β+λ′_k (N′ depends on x), outside the companion and Bernoulli-column spans;
   - LD0 (order43): exact for the k = 0 part only, with M_b and weight 1/x;
   - the order45 symmetry: relates x and 1/x, not x and −1/x.
   None of these yields a fixed-matrix law in the 54-dim span without fitting.
3. A matrix chosen to minimise the residual would be invented and fitted, which the instruction forbids.
## Decision
No test is run in order46. The package documents the obstruction. Weight variants (x^{−w}) were covered by the order31–34 family and give no exact law. No numerics are needed.
Naming: RG* only, no labels L1–L7; "36" only as dim V. MAPS_CANDIDATES untouched; no Resonant objects; no new objects; no bridge claim.
