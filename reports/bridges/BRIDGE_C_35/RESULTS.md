## Map B: the vector-valued S-law for RTA's C at leading order (new Category A theorem)
**Lemma 2 (radial asymptotics; full proof of Residual-Law A2 Lemma 3).** For y = h/m and t → 0+:
t·P_orth(e(y)e^{−t}) = I·G(y) + O(t^{1/2}), t·H_7(e(y)e^{−t}) = O(t^{1/2}), hence t·C(e(y)e^{−t}) = I·G(y) + O(t^{1/2}), with I = log(6+√35)/(2√35).
*Proof.* (i) Sum–integral lemma. Let K be a closed sector in R², Q a real quadratic form with Q(v) ≥ c|v|² on a closed sector K_δ containing K \ {0} in its interior (c > 0), ℓ linear, f: Z² → C N-periodic, φ_t(v) = e^{−tQ(v)}. Fix a residue r. Tile R² by the cells N u + r + [0,N)², u ∈ Z², with base point p = r + Nu. For a cell inside K: |φ_t(p) − N^{−2}∫_cell φ_t| ≤ √2 N·sup_cell|∇φ_t|. On the cell, |∇φ_t| = t|∇Q|e^{−tQ} ≤ C t(|p|+1)e^{−tQ(v)}, and Q(v) ≥ Q(p) − C(|p|+1) ≥ c|p|² − C(|p|+1), so e^{−tQ(v)} ≤ C′e^{−tc|p|²/2} for t ≤ 1 (the quadratic c|p|²/2 − C(|p|+1) is bounded below). Summing over p in a lattice of covolume N²: Σ_p t(|p|+1)e^{−tc|p|²/2} = O(t·t^{−3/2}) = O(t^{−1/2}). Cells meeting ∂K: at most O(R) of them have |p| ≤ R, each term and each cell integral is ≤ C e^{−tc|p|²/2} (they lie in K_δ for |p| large), giving O(Σ_R e^{−tcR²/2}) = O(t^{−1/2}). So Σ_{p∈(r+NZ²)∩K} φ_t(p) = N^{−2}∫_K φ_t + O(t^{−1/2}), and ∫_K φ_t = t^{−1}∫_K e^{−Q} (substitute v = u/√t). Multiplying by f(r) and summing over r: Σ_{v∈K∩Z²} f(v)φ_t(v) = (mean f)·t^{−1}∫_K e^{−Q} + O(t^{−1/2}). Replacing Q by Q+ℓ: |e^{−t(Q+ℓ)} − e^{−tQ}| ≤ t|ℓ(v)|e^{−tc|v|²/2} for |v| ≥ R₀, whose sum is O(t·t^{−3/2}) = O(t^{−1/2}). Changing the weights on the two boundary rays changes the sum by O(Σ_r e^{−tcr²}) = O(t^{−1/2}).
(ii) Orthant. K = [0,∞)², Q(n,k) = n² + 7nk + (7/2)k² ≥ n² + (7/2)k² ≥ |v|² on K (and on a slightly larger sector). Q_7 = Q + (7/2)k, f(v) = e(yQ_7(v)) is 2m-periodic (Q_7 is integer-valued, and Q_7(v + 2m e_i) − Q_7(v) ∈ mZ: the differences are 4mn + 4m² + 14mk and 14mn + 14mk + 14m² + 7m). mean f = G(y). ∫_K e^{−Q} = ∫_0^∞ dλ/(2Q(1,λ)) (polar substitution along rays (1,λ), then ∫_0^∞ e^{−ρ²Q}ρ dρ = 1/(2Q)); Q(1,λ) = (7/2)(λ − r₁)(λ − r₂) with r₁,₂ = (−7 ± √35)/7, so the integral is (1/(2√35))·log(r₂/r₁) = (1/(2√35))·log((7+√35)/(7−√35)) = log(6+√35)/(2√35) = I.
(iii) Cone. B(c1,v) = 5n and B(c2,v) = −n − 21k, so w(v) = ½(sgn n + sgn(n+21k)). w = +1 on the interior of K₊ = {n ≥ 0, n + 21k ≥ 0}, w = −1 on the interior of K₋ = −K₊, w = ±½ on the boundary rays, 0 elsewhere. K₊ is spanned by u₁ = (21,−1) and u₂ = (0,1), and Q(au₁+bu₂) = 297.5a² + 140ab + 3.5b² is positive definite for a, b ≥ 0, so (i) applies to K₊ and (with ℓ' = −(7/2)k, f'(u) = e(yQ_7(−u))) to K₋ via v = −u. The two leading terms are G(y)·t^{−1}∫_{K₊}e^{−Q} and −mean(f')·t^{−1}∫_{K₊}e^{−Q}; mean(f') = mean_{u mod 2m} e(yQ_7(−u)) = G(y), since u ↦ −u permutes Z²/2mZ². They cancel. ∎
