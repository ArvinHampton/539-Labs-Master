- **Untested variant.** The preregistered family used coefficients ε_Q ∈ {0,1}. A completion with non-trivial, analytically derived coefficients was not tested: for example, erf-completion constants that depend on the angle between the wall and the null directions of the indefinite form. That is the natural refinement. It needs the constants derived from the actual completion of H_7 first, then preregistered.
