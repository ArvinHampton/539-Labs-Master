phase, where the newly formed black hole settles into a stable state by emitting gravitational

GW241110 have precisely measured multiple modes in the ringdown signals, confirming general
relativity predictions about black hole mass and spin. These modes resemble the decaying
vibrations of a struck bell, with frequencies and damping rates aligning closely with theoretical
models. However, searches for additional echoes—potential signatures of physics beyond
general relativity, such as quantum effects near the horizon or modified horizons—have yielded
limited evidence. For instance, a 2025 study examining 47 merger events found no broad

continue to test alternative theories, with no conclusive detections yet across the
