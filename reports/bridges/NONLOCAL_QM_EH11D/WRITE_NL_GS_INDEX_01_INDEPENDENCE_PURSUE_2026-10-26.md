## 3. Lemmas toward independence
Notation: g_s = Σ_n T_n with T_n = q^{n²}/∏_{k≤n}(1+q^{sk}), as in FINAL §3.

### Lemma I1 (PROOF): the three series agree mod q^8.
For s∈{7,8,11}, g_s ≡ 1 + q + q^4 (mod q^8).
*Proof.* T_0 = 1. T_1 = q(1 − q^s + …) ≡ q, because 1+s ≥ 8. T_2 = q^4(1 − q^s − … ) ≡ q^4, because 4+s ≥ 11. For n≥3, T_n has degree ≥ 9. ∎
(A box machine check gives 1+q+q^4 through q^7 for all three series. This is a SAMPLE confirmation.)

### Lemma I2 (PROOF): the differences have rank 2.
D₁ = g_7 − g_8 and D₂ = g_8 − g_11 are linearly independent over ℚ.
*Proof.* [q^8]D₁ = −1 and [q^8]D₂ = 0 (G1). [q^9]D₂ = 0 − 1 = −1. If aD₁ + bD₂ = 0, the q^8 coefficient gives a = 0, and then the q^9 coefficient gives b = 0. ∎

### Lemma I3 (PROOF): characterisation of linear independence.
Let X^M be independent of s, and let Φ be ℚ-linear on the ℚ[[q]]-module generated by the g_s X^M. Then Φ(g_s X^M) is independent of s∈{7,8,11} **iff** Φ(D₁X^M) = Φ(D₂X^M) = 0. By I2 this is a codimension-2 condition on Φ restricted to span{g_s X^M}. Once X^M is a non-zero-divisor, the span is 3-dimensional, so Φ must factor through a 1-dimensional quotient that kills the 2-dimensional span of D₁X^M and D₂X^M.
*Proof.* Linearity together with I2 and G2 of `1330e8f2…`. ∎

### Lemma I4 (PROOF): a sufficient condition from truncation.
If Φ factors through reduction mod q^8 (more generally, mod q^N with N ≤ 8), and X^M ∈ ℚ[[q]] does not depend on s, then Φ(g_s X^M) is independent of s.
*Proof.* By I1, (g_s − g_{s'}) X^M ∈ q^8 ℚ[[q]], and this ideal lies in the kernel of the reduction. ∎
**Analytic corollary (PROOF):** for |q| ≤ r < 1 and bounded analytic X^M, |g_s X^M − g_{s'} X^M| = O(r^8) uniformly. In the q→0 regime, any continuous Φ is s-independent up to O(q^8).

### Lemma I5 (PROOF, negative): there is no non-trivial "full-series" independence.
If Φ is injective modulo q^9 on span{g_s X^M} and X^M is a unit with X^M(0) ≠ 0, then Φ depends on s.
*Proof.* D₁X^M ≡ −X^M(0)q^8 ≢ 0 (mod q^9). ∎
In words: independence must come from truncation or a kernel. It cannot come from the full series.
