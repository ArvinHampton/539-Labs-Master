## Near-misses (explicitly NOT a direct single-MT torsional-rigidity measurement)

### A. Single-MT experiments that are NOT torsion of the lattice

1. **Kis et al., Phys. Rev. Lett. 89, 248101 (2002)** — PMID 12484982; DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.89.248101.  
   AFM elastic deformation of a single microtubule on a lithographed substrate; reports Young’s and shear moduli anisotropy. PubMed abstract states (exact): “The shear modulus is 2 orders of magnitude lower than the Young's”.  
   **NOT** a twist / rotational-tracking / cryo-EM thermal-torsion measurement of torsional rigidity.

2. **Kang, Serey, Chen & Erickson — nanophotonic tweezers — Nano Lett. (2012)** — PMID 23145817; DOI 10.1021/nl303747n.  
   Rotates single microtubules in an optical angular trap. Abstract states (exact): “An angular trap stiffness of κ = 92.8 pN·nm/rad(2)·mW is demonstrated for the microtubules”.  
   **NOT** the microtubule’s intrinsic torsional rigidity — this is the *trap* angular stiffness.

3. Extensive optical-tweezers / thermal-fluctuation literature measures **flexural** rigidity of single MTs (e.g. Gittes et al., J. Cell Biol. 120:923–934, 1993 — PMID 8432732; DOI 10.1083/jcb.120.4.923), not torsional rigidity. Not enumerated exhaustively here because flexural ≠ torsional.

### B. Bundle / axoneme / spindle — NOT single-MT

4. **Novak et al., Nat. Commun. 9:3571 (2018)** — PMID 30177685; DOI 10.1038/s41467-018-06005-7.  
   **NOT single-MT.** Live/fixed spindle imaging of microtubule *bundles*; model fit of shapes. Paper states (exact abstract phrasing): “We found that bundles turn by about −2 deg µm−1 around the spindle axis, which we explain by a twisting moment of roughly −10 pNµm.” Full text reports for 52 bundles: twisting moment “−8.4 ± 0.8 pNµm” and bending moment “139 ± 7 pNµm”. Authors note quantification of torque components is **indirect** (fit to shapes). Model includes torsional rigidity τ as a parameter, but the fitted experimental outputs reported are **moments (torques) on bundles**, not a measured single-MT torsional rigidity.

5. **Lazarus, Soheilypour & Mofrad, Biophys. J. 109:231–239 (2015)** — PMID 26200859; DOI 10.1016/j.bpj.2015.06.029.  
   **NOT single-MT; NOT experiment.** Coarse-grained *computational* model of axonal MT *bundles* under applied torsion. No experimental torsional-rigidity value for a single MT.

6. **Lindemann & Lesich, Cytoskeleton 81:648–668 (2024)** — PMID 38780123; DOI 10.1002/cm.21879.  
   Review of axoneme mechanics. Web-verified synthesis of the full text (Wiley): states that **direct measurement of the torsional resistance of the intact axoneme remains to be accomplished**. **NOT single-MT;** reinforces absence of a measured axonemal torsional-resistance value.

7. **Striegler et al., Nat. Phys. (2025)** — DOI 10.1038/s41567-025-02783-2 (Crossref verified; no PubMed PMID located via NCBI esearch on the DOI this beat).  
   **NOT single-MT.** Experimental 3D waveform / gold-nanoparticle tracking of *Chlamydomonas* axonemes showing twist–torsion coupling of the beating axoneme. Reports geometric torsion of the waveform (publisher abstract / page: torsion-wave observations), **not** a torsional-rigidity/stiffness modulus of a single microtubule.

### C. Theory / simulation / continuum estimates — excluded by brief (already known)

8. **Chełminiak, Dixon & Tuszyński, Eur. Phys. J. E 31:215–227 (2010)** — PMID 20221665; DOI 10.1140/epje/i2010-10562-x.  
   Continuous-sheet theory; abstract: “We estimate the value of torsional rigidity for microtubules based on their structure and some experimentally known elastic properties.” **Theory/estimate — not counted.**

9. **Sept & MacKintosh, Phys. Rev. Lett. 104:018101 (2010)** — PMID 20366396; DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.104.018101.  
   All-atom MD connected to continuum mechanics (Young’s / persistence length). **Simulation/continuum — not counted** as a direct torsion experiment.

### D. Method exists for actin (contrast only)

10. **Tsuda et al., Proc. Natl Acad. Sci. USA 93:12937–12942 (1996)** — PMID 8917522; DOI 10.1073/pnas.93.23.12937.  
    Direct *in vitro* micromanipulation of **single actin** filaments. Abstract states (exact): torsional rigidity “(8.0 ± 1.2) × 10−26 Nm2”.  
    **Actin, not microtubule.** Cited only to show the experimental class exists for another cytoskeletal filament and was not located for MT.
