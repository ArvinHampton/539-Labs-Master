## 1. 2007 file (fixes F1–F11)
- **E19-1 (REFUTED, by PP's rank count; F2).** 2007 B4's claim that "a BPS wall would make **4** conditions dependent" is refuted.
  - Unknowns at the wall, with A and r_w quotiented: (φ_w, λ_w, φ′_w, λ′_w, A′_w), so **5**.
