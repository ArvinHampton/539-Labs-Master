- **N6. I1 hypothesis and counterexample.**
  - **E17-N6a (PROVED, given the hypothesis).** I1 holds for any static wall field with **no x-legs**. In general form, T^a_a − T^x_x = |F(e_a,·)|² ≥ 0 for each Σ unit vector e_a. Mixed Σ–fibre legs add only a non-negative amount. Smeared M5s, S = −σ P_wv, are isotropic on x ⊕ Σ and cancel. The hypothesis is 4D Poincaré invariance of the wall field: on ℝ^{1,3} there is no invariant 2-form or vector, so F_{μν} = F_{μi} = 0. I2 needs no such hypothesis (it holds for every YM field, x-legs included, at σ ≥ 0).

## 2. What Y15 implies for the breathing route (OW 90cec14c)

- **E17-NEC (PROVED; NL checked PP's corollary).** NEC along ℓ_v = t̂ + v requires k_v ≤ k_x pointwise for every unit wall-spatial direction v, Σ and fibre directions included. At the odd HW-type ℤ₂ wall, IR side kept, the Israel tensor is S_ab = −(2/κ²)(K_ab − h_ab K), so S(ℓ_v, ℓ_v) = −(2/κ²)(k_v − k_x). For NEC-respecting wall content, S(ℓ_v, ℓ_v) ≥ 0, hence k_v ≤ k_x. For E₈ fields, I1 is exactly the Σ case of this.
- **E17-NEC-IR (PROVED).** On the unperturbed MN2 IR face, k_Σ = k_fib = 0 > k_x = −k at every point of S⁴. Every NEC-satisfying wall content therefore fails there, with margin k. This is a pointwise statement: the stage-2 result that the count gap closes in magnitude does not help, and no combination of E₈ fields (any charge, single blocks, mirror pairs, twisted or not) with σ ≥ 0 smeared M5s can close the junction on this face.
- Consequently, a breathing-mode candidate under OW 90cec14c must either
  - make k_v ≤ k_x pointwise on S⁴ for every Σ and fibre direction v, or
  - bring an NEC-violating source (σ < 0 M5s, G₄ / worldvolume flux of the right sign, or a tr R² stress far larger than Y16's bound).
  - The alternatives are a different face, or keeping the UV side (which needs negative wall energy).
- The HW tr R² term is the only listed ingredient that can be negative; on the integrated bound it is ~10³ too small. A per-direction check is still owed (Y15 §7).
