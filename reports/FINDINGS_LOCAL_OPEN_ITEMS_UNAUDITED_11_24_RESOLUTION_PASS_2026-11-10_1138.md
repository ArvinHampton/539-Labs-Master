## 1. R4b: Jac(Bolza) has CM by ℚ(√−2). Local proof from the curve

Let C: y² = f(x) = x⁵ − x (genus 2; the roots of f are 0, ±1, ±i and ∞). All facts below are exact (log B1).

**1.1 An extra involution.** Let m(x) = (x+1)/(x−1). Then m∘m = id, and f(m(x)) = 8 f(x)/(x−1)⁶ (log: difference 0). Hence
 s(x, y) = ( (x+1)/(x−1), 2√2·y/(x−1)³ )
is an automorphism of C defined over ℚ(√2), with s² = id (log: "s² on y: y"). It is neither the identity nor ι: (x, y) ↦ (x, −y), since it moves x.

**1.2 Action on differentials.** On the basis (dx/y, x dx/y) of H⁰(C, Ω¹), s* has matrix (√2/2)·[[1, −1], [−1, −1]], with eigenvalues +1 and −1, each once (log). Because ι* = −1 on H⁰(Ω¹), the involution sι has the opposite eigenvalues. So E₊ := C/⟨s⟩ and E₋ := C/⟨sι⟩ have genus = dim of the invariant differentials = 1. Both are elliptic curves, and neither quotient map is constant.

**1.3 Decomposition.** On J = Jac(C), ι acts as −1 (R4d), so sι acts as −s. Put A± := (1 ± s)J. Then 2 = (1+s) + (1−s) gives A₊ + A₋ = J. Also (1+s)(1−s) = 1 − s² = 0, so A₊ ⊆ ker(1−s) and A₋ ⊆ ker(1+s), and every x ∈ A₊ ∩ A₋ satisfies 2x = 0. The tangent space of A± is the ±1 eigenspace of s on H⁰(Ω¹)^∨, which is 1-dimensional by 1.2. A₊ is the image of Jac(E₊) under pullback by C → E₊, and A₋ is the image of Jac(E₋) by the same construction with sι. Hence the addition map A₊ × A₋ → J is an isogeny, and **J ~ E₊ × E₋** over ℚ(√2). PROVED (elementary).

**1.4 The two factors.** Use the coordinate t = (x − α)/(x − β) with α = 1+√2 and β = 1−√2, the two fixed points of m. Then m acts as t ↦ −t (log: check 0). Write Y = y(1−t)³.
