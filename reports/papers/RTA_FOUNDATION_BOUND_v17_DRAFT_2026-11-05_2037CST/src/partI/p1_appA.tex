\subsection{Algebraic checks (Sections 3--7)}\label{algebraic-checks-sections-37}

\texttt{P/code/gklib.py} evaluates \(g_k(d)\) exactly (Python integers). \texttt{P/code/verify\_algebra.py} (log: \texttt{P/data/verify\_algebra\_log.txt}) checks:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  stabilisation, sign rule, \(c_m=(-1)^mQ(m)\), the product form (4.1) and the truncation formula (Lemmas 4.1--4.5), for \(n\le80\), \(m\le400\);
\item
  the expansion (3.1) against the direct series (\(k\le20\), \(k=27,33\), \(d\le1500\));
\item
  Theorem 5.1 for all \(k\le1001\) (\(608\) squarefree \(k\): all vanish; \(392\) non-squarefree \(k\): all nonzero);
\item
  formula (5.1) in \(5121\) cases and the exact failures of Corollary 5.4 for all odd prime powers \(k\le1001\);
\item
  the pair reduction (6.1) for \(7\le k\le130\) and the values \(g_{15}(286)=g_{21}(526)=0\);
\item
  Theorem 7.7 for even \(k\le130\) (values of \(g_{2^a}\));
\item
  the sign law and the zero set of \(g_2\) for \(d\le6000\); zero sets for \(k=2,6,10,14\) and the bounds \(B_c\).
\end{itemize}

The checks are consistency tests of the proved statements, not parts of proofs, except the exact term lists quoted in proofs (Corollary 5.4, Sections 6.6, 7.2, 7.3), which are exact integer evaluations. \texttt{P/code/even\_zero\_sets.py} produces Table B.2 (\texttt{P/data/even\_zero\_sets\_k2mod4.txt}).

Run: \texttt{cd\ P/code\ \&\&\ python3\ verify\_algebra.py} (about 5 seconds).
