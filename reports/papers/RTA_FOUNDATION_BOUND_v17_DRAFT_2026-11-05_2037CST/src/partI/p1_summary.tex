\textbf{Scope.} Only proved statements are included. For even \(k\) no claim is made on classes of \(d\) that are not one-sign. For odd \(k\ge35\) no claim is made beyond Sections 4--6.

\textbf{Dependence on external results.} Theorem 6.9 for \(s\ge2501\) and the monotonicity of \(Q(n)\) for \(n\ge35200\) (Lemma 6.5) rely on the explicit bound of Chern \cite{Chern} (Theorem~1.1, error term (3.10), Remark~3), cited in Section 6.3. Computer-assisted steps use the Arb ball arithmetic \cite{Arb} and exact integer arithmetic; they are listed in Section 8.8 and Appendix A.
