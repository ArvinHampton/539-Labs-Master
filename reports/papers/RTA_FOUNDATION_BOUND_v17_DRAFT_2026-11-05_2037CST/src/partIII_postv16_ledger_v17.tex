\subsection{Status vocabulary used below}
\noindent In Sections~\ref{sec:postv16}--\ref{sec:boundaryrank} and Appendix~\ref{app:withdrawn-v17} the labels mean:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{PROVED}: a complete proof is written from stated hypotheses;
\item \textbf{CONDITIONAL}: the statement is proved only under explicitly named extra hypotheses;
\item \textbf{OPEN}: neither proved nor refuted on the present record;
\item \textbf{REFUTED}: a counterexample or inconsistency is recorded against the stated claim.
\end{itemize}
Absence of familiarity with a construction is not a refutation. (This vocabulary is editorial; it is not a numbered mathematical definition.)
