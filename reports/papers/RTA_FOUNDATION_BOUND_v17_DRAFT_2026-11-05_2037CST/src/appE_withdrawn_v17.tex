\section{Withdrawn or excluded proposals (Version~17 DRAFT)}\label{app:withdrawn-v17}
This short appendix records proposals that are \emph{not} part of the proved core of Sections~\ref{sec:postv16}--\ref{sec:boundaryrank}. It is not a development of those proposals.

\begin{proposition}[Clifford and quantum readings excluded from the proved core]\label{prop:v17-clifford-out}
The following are excluded from the Category~A content of Section~\ref{sec:boundaryrank}:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*)]
\item automatic identification of the $48$-dimensional regular representation with a Clifford algebra $\mathrm{Cl}$;
\item any proposed inversion map $q$ that fails translation invariance on~$G$;
\item an action of $\mathrm{Aut}(G)$ extending automorphically along a map $\Q G\to\mathrm{Cl}$ without a written construction;
\item any entanglement or confinement reading of $N$ and $W$ beyond the path-mass inequalities of Definition~\ref{def:v17-NW}.
\end{enumerate}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
These items lack written proofs in the reviewed Addenda~A--X stream as integrated here; listing them prevents silent upgrade. Absence of a construction is not itself a refutation of every future Clifford model.
\end{proof}
\catA{Excluded from proved core; not a long speculative development.}

\begin{proposition}[Undefined modular--Iwasawa bridges]\label{prop:v17-undefined-bridges}
Assignments of the form ``matrix kernel $\Rightarrow$ mock-modular shadow'' or ``matrix kernel $\Rightarrow$ Iwasawa $\mathcal L$-invariant profile'' remain UNDEFINED or CONDITIONAL until a verified bridge is written. No fake bridge is asserted in this draft.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Addendum~W and the Auditor OPEN inventory: the mapping is missing.
\end{proof}
\catB{OPEN/UNDEFINED. No automatic upgrade; no PROVED status.}
