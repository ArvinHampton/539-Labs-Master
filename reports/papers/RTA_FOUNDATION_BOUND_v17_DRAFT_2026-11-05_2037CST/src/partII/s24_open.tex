\section{Open problems}\label{sec:open}
%=====================================================================
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*.,leftmargin=2em]
\item Prove the cusp-row identities of Theorem~\ref{thm:K} (currently Category~B).
\item Prove the symmetry $\psi(-x)=\overline{\psi(x)}$ (observed numerically to $8\cdot10^{-14}$; not used).
\item An arithmetic (Gauss-sum or ideal-theoretic) proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:J}.
\item Whether Zwegers' completed theta transforms under the same $140$-dimensional $\rho$ (Remark~\ref{rem:struct}(e)); whether an exotic single kernel produces both sectors of $\Cthird$.
\item Objects whose cocycles are of size $\ge1/y$ are not excluded by Theorems~\ref{thm:O1}, \ref{thm:O2}.
\item $\dim V=s(s-1)$ for all odd $s$ (the lower bound); equality $d_{\rm Eis}=3\prod(p^e+1)/2$ in general; $d_2(k,v)$ for $k\ge7$.
\item Sharpness of the Proposition~K1 bounds for $m=2k^2-1$ with $k\ge6$ and on the full space ($\dim V(578)\le83532$); composite $m$ and $g$-consistency for all odd $m$ in Proposition~\ref{prop:hc5}.
\item A uniform proof of the prime-square decompositions; (C3) for general $p$ without citation; irreducibility of the complements at $s=121$ and $s=169$; the label-space decomposition at $s=3481$ (whose $p$-local invariants are out of reach in label space).
\item The ambiguous-class count $|\bar G^\sigma|=2^{\omega(s)}\varphi(s-2)$ in general; sufficiency of the modulus $M=2dc$ for all norms.
\item The pattern of $S_e$ for all odd $e$; the identity $R_r=S^{\rm ax}_r$ at general order; closed forms at general rationals; the $t^{19/2}$ coefficient and beyond.
\item A period-function correspondence for $\rho$ at $\lambda=\tfrac14$ that also covers the non-cusp element $u_{\rm lift}$ (Remark~\ref{rem:uniqB}(a)).
\item Whether $\gamma$ itself lifts on the rank-$6$ Noether--Lefschetz locus of Proposition~\ref{prop:liftgamma}(iii) (Remark~\ref{rem:open8}(b)); any link between Noether--Lefschetz series and the residual objects beyond the shared representation.
\item Coefficient-emitting maps outside the classes of \S\ref{sec:emit} (Remark~\ref{rem:N1scope}).
\item Quantum modularity of scalar $C$ and of $g_s$ (not claimed).
\end{enumerate}
